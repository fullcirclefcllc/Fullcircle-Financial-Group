## Succession & Legacy Planning

### Who It's For
Clients with $500K+ in assets or business owners who need a documented succession plan.

### What It Includes
- Review of current trust structure
- Business succession agreement (buy-sell agreement, key person analysis)
- Life insurance adequacy review for estate funding
- Beneficiary designation audit across all accounts
- Letter of instruction (for heirs and family)
- Coordination with estate attorney partner

### Deliverable
A 10-page "Legacy Blueprint" document outlining: current asset picture, succession plan, trust/estate structure, insurance adequacy, and 5-year implementation roadmap.

## Business Exit Strategy

### Who It's For
Business owner clients planning an exit within 5 years.

## Scale SOPs

### Activating the Scale Pillar with a Client

1. Confirm all three prior pillars are in motion (Protection in place, Structure filed, Leverage building)
2. Present the Fractional CFO retainer at the 90-day milestone review call
3. Assess tier based on complexity and income: Foundation ($750) / Blueprint ($1,500) / Done With You ($3,000)
4. Set up monthly review cadence in Calendly
5. Send welcome email with monthly deliverables template
6. Add to Architect's Circle at appropriate access level

### Annual Review Protocol

Every active retainer client receives an annual financial architecture review:
1. Full financial snapshot update
2. Net worth comparison (year-over-year)
3. Entity structure review
4. Insurance adequacy check
5. Tax strategy optimization with CPA
6. Goals reset for next 12 months

Deliver as a written report. This is also the renewal and upsell moment.
