# Client Onboarding Workflow

**FullCircle Financial Group**

---

## Phase 1 — Discovery

| Step | Action | Owner | Tool / Template |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Initial inquiry received | Breyon | — |
| 2 | Send pre-call questionnaire or schedule discovery call | Breyon | Calendly / email |
| 3 | Complete discovery call (30 min) | Breyon | — |
| 4 | Assess fit and recommended pathway | Breyon | — |
| 5 | Send proposal / package recommendation | Breyon | `05_Templates-and-Proposals/proposals/` |

## Phase 2 — Enrollment

| Step | Action | Owner | Tool / Template |
|---|---|---|---|
| 6 | Client selects package | Client | — |
| 7 | Send service agreement | Breyon | `02_Clients/_templates/service-agreement.md` |
| 8 | Collect signed agreement + deposit | Breyon | — |
| 9 | Send intake form | Breyon | `02_Clients/_templates/client-intake-form.md` |
| 10 | Create client folder in `02_Clients/[LastName-FirstName]/` | Breyon | — |

## Phase 3 — Onboarding Call

| Step | Action | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| 11 | Schedule onboarding call (60 min) | Breyon |
| 12 | Complete financial snapshot together | Breyon + Client |
| 13 | Present recommended structure (entity diagram) | Breyon |
| 14 | Set 30/60/90 day milestones | Breyon |
| 15 | Begin Pillar 1 (entity filing) | Breyon |

## Phase 4 — Delivery

- Execute SOPs for each pillar per client's package
- Update progress tracker at each milestone
- Send milestone update to client via email

## Phase 5 — Review & Retention

| Timeline | Action |
|---|---|
| 30 days | Check-in call — entity status, bank account, D-U-N-S |
| 90 days | Review call — credit profile, IUL status, next steps |
| 6 months | Mid-year review — all pillars, adjust as needed |
| 12 months | Annual review — full financial picture, upsell / referral ask |

## Post-Discovery Follow-Up Sequence (3-Touch)

**Trigger:** Discovery call completed, proposal not yet signed  
**Owner:** SalesCloser agent or Breyon  
**SLA:** Touch 1 within 24 hours of call

### Touch 1 — Day 1 (Same Day / Next Morning)

**Subject:** Your Financial Architecture Blueprint — [CLIENT NAME]

```
[Name],

Great conversation yesterday. Based on what you shared — [1 specific pain point] — I've attached your custom proposal.

The key finding: you're likely leaving $[X]–$[X] on the table annually without the right structure.

Proposal attached. Valid for 7 days.

Ready to move? Reply "let's go" and I'll send the agreement + deposit link.

— Breyon
The Financial Architect
FullCircle Financial Group
```

### Touch 2 — Day 3

**Subject:** Quick question about your proposal

```
[Name],

Wanted to check — did you get a chance to review the proposal?

The [QBI permanence / tax quarter / slow season] timing makes [specific pillar] especially urgent right now.

Happy to jump on a 15-min call if you have questions.

— Breyon
```

### Touch 3 — Day 7 (Value-Add)

**Subject:** Thought this might help — [relevant resource]

```
[Name],

Even if timing isn't right yet, this might be useful:

[Link to case study / tax tip / 4-pillar one-pager]

When you're ready to stop leaving $[X]/yr on the table, the proposal is still open: [link]

No pressure — just want you to have the resource.

— Breyon
```

**After Touch 3 with no response:** Move to nurture in lead-pipeline.md. Re-engage at 30 days.
