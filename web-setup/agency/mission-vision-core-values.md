## Core Values

### 1. Protection First
Every recommendation starts with what happens *before* the claim, the lawsuit, or the tax bill. We train agents to lead with protection — term for immediate need, whole life for permanence, IUL for living benefits — not product quotas.

### 2. Structure Over Chaos
Agencies fail when everyone runs their own system. We provide one operating architecture: pipelines, fields, workflows, and dashboards that scale from 3 agents to 300 without reinventing the wheel.

### 3. Leverage Through Systems
Technology is not a cost center — it is leverage. CRM, automations, illustration workflows, and training portals exist so agents spend time in front of clients, not inside spreadsheets.

### 4. Scale With Integrity
Growth without compliance is a liability. Every workflow embeds suitability standards, licensing checks, and documentation trails. We scale agencies the way carriers expect: clean books, strong persistency, ethical sales.

### 5. Transparency Always
Commission structures, vesting schedules, KPI targets, and carrier appointments are documented in plain language. Agents and agency partners never discover the rules after they sign.

### 6. Education Before Execution
We do not hand agents a script and a lead list. We certify them product-by-product — Term Foundations, Whole Life Architecture, IUL Mastery — before they present complex products to clients.
