## Brand Voice Principles

1. **Premium, not pretentious** — sophisticated but approachable
2. **Problem-first** — lead with the client's pain, not your credentials
3. **Direct** — short sentences, no filler
4. **Empowering** — the client is the protagonist; you are the architect
5. **Evidence-based** — back claims with real client outcomes

## Key Proof Points
- Client: From $40K in taxes → IUL + trust + LLC in 60 days
- Client: $120,000 in business credit in 90 days, no personal guarantee
