**Hook 2 — The SE Tax Problem**
> "If you are self-employed and filing as a sole proprietor or a single-member LLC, you are paying 15.3% in self-employment tax on every dollar you earn — on top of income tax. At $200,000 income, that's $30,000+ in SE tax alone. An S-Corp election on your LLC can legally cut that number in half. I've been showing clients this for 30 years. It is one of the most overlooked legal tax strategies in existence."

**Hook 3 — The QBI Deduction**
> "If you own a pass-through business — an LLC, S-Corp, or partnership — you may qualify for a 20–23% deduction on your qualified business income. That means on $200,000 in business income, you could deduct $40,000–$46,000 before taxes are calculated. Most 1099 earners have no idea this exists. Our tax strategist makes sure every eligible client captures it — every single year."

**Hook 4 — The All-In-One Advantage**

Your LLC strategy doesn't account for your tax bracket. Your estate plan doesn't align with your retirement strategy. At FullCircle, our in-house tax strategist works directly with your entire financial blueprint. Everything is coordinated. Nothing falls through the cracks."
