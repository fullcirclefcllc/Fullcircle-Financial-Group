Questions

What agents ask first

Does signing up change my 1099 status?

No. Every plan is voluntary and in your own name, and you pay for it yourself. Barrett doesn't sponsor, choose, or pay for your coverage, so your status as an independent contractor stays the same.

Will I get approved? Do I need a medical exam?

You won't need an exam, a blood draw or a doctor visit. The supplemental and worksite life plans are simplified issue: you answer a few health questions, and if your answers are no, you're approved. When you enroll as part of the Barrett group, those plans are guaranteed issue during the enrollment window, so everyone qualifies, within each plan's benefit limits and age bands. Individual life and IUL policies are underwritten, which is how you lock in a rate while you're healthy.

What happens if I leave Barrett?

You keep your coverage. You own your policies, so they stay with you and so does your rate.

I already have a health plan. Do I need this?

Your health plan pays the doctor and the hospital. It doesn't pay your deductible, your mortgage, or your bills during the weeks you can't work. Supplemental plans pay you cash for exactly that. Use the calculator above to see your own numbers.

What does this cost Barrett, and what does it cost me?

Barrett pays nothing. You pay only for the plans you choose, at group rates, by your own draft. The education program and your reviews are free, and you're never required to buy anything.

Is the dental, vision and telehealth membership insurance?

No. It's a discount membership that lowers what you pay for care. It is not insurance, and we'll always describe it that way.
