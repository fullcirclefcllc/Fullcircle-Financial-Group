The front door

The Launch flow: everything needed to file, in 7 steps.

Built and tested in the app; the same questions are in the GHL Launch form so you can sell it now.

  1. 1
    StructureTwo questions, then a live recommendation: add a Wyoming holding company or start with one LLC.
  2. 2
    NamesState, ending, company name, two backups, holding company name, industry, what the business does.
  3. 3
    OwnersNames and percentages (must total 100%), who manages it, and what will show on the public record.
  4. 4
    Addresses and agentBusiness address, registered agents in both states, contact for documents.
  5. 5
    Taxes and EINTax election, start date, employees. SSN collected later on an encrypted form.
  6. 6
    Phone and domainLocal or toll-free line, domain, professional inbox.
  7. 7
    Review and launchReady-to-file checklist and the full list of what gets filed for both companies.
THE PRIVACY STRUCTURE
YouOwner · kept off public filings
Holdings LLC · WyomingOwners not listed; agent is organizer and address of record
Operating LLC · ArizonaRuns the business; lists the holding company as its owner

Arizona lists owners publicly. Privacy covers public records only. Banks, the IRS and courts can still see ownership.

Built-in guardrails

Compliance isn't a policy binder. It's in the code.

Each rule below is enforced by the GHL workflows, the app, or the database itself.

Workflow + app3-day cancellation windowNo credit work or billing until the window passes without a cancellation.
DatabasePay after serviceA credit repair invoice is refused until its round is sent, and only on the high-risk processor.
DatabaseFunding fee capRefused before funding, and above $10,000.
AppEncrypted SSNsNever in GHL. Encrypted before storage, visible only to admins and processors, every access logged.
AI guideNo promises, no adviceBlocked from guarantees, CPNs, legal or tax advice, and claiming it filed anything. Upset clients go to a strategist.
App + agreementsLicensed-only insuranceProtection work goes only to licensed agents; no insurance revenue to unlicensed partners.
