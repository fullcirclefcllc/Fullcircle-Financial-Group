# Whole Life Pipeline

**Pipeline Name:** Whole Life  
**GHL Location:** Opportunities → Pipelines → Whole Life  
**Access:** Whole Life Certified agents only

---

## Stages

| # | Stage | Definition | Max Days | Auto-Action |
|---|-------|------------|----------|-------------|
| 1 | **New Lead** | Lead identified for permanent coverage | 1 | Tag `product:whole`, assign certified agent |
| 2 | **Contacted** | Initial conversation held | 2 | Nurture if no response |
| 3 | **Discovery Scheduled** | Financial discovery call booked | 3 | Pre-call financial snapshot form |
| 4 | **Needs Analysis Complete** | Permanent need documented | 3 | Suitability checklist complete |
| 5 | **Illustration Requested** | Carrier illustration ordered | 5 | Set `illustration_requested=true` |
| 6 | **Illustration Presented** | Client reviewed guaranteed + non-guaranteed columns | 7 | Focus on CSV timeline, policy loans |
| 7 | **Quote Accepted** | Client committed to proceed | 3 | Application workflow |
| 8 | **App Submitted** | Application to carrier | 14 | Set `app_submitted_date` |
| 9 | **Underwriting** | Medical/financial underwriting | 28 | Client update workflow |
| 10 | **Placed** | In-force permanent policy | — | Book of business, persistency tracking |
| 11 | **Lost** | Did not proceed | — | Lost reason required |

---

## Whole Life Specific Requirements

Before Illustration Requested:
- [ ] Client primary goal = permanent protection OR estate OR cash value
- [ ] Premium budget confirmed ($300+/mo typical minimum)
- [ ] Existing term coverage reviewed (replace vs supplement)
- [ ] Health pre-screen completed

Before App Submitted:
- [ ] Illustration signed/acknowledged by client
- [ ] Suitability documentation on file
- [ ] Beneficiary design complete
- [ ] Agent appointed with selected carrier

---

## Illustration Review Checklist (Agent)

Present to client:
1. Guaranteed death benefit
2. Guaranteed cash value (Year 5, 10, 20)
3. Non-guaranteed illustrated values (stress-test assumptions)
4. Policy loan mechanics and impact on death benefit
5. Premium payment options (limited pay vs lifetime)
6. What happens if premium missed

**Red flags to catch before client sees illustration:**
- Illustrated rate exceeds carrier cap
- Cash value access before Year 3–5 without disclosure
- Premium financing implications not discussed

## Cross-Sell Triggers

| Client Profile | Upsell Conversation |
|----------------|---------------------|
| Has term only | "Convert portion to permanent before term expires" |
| Business owner | Key person / buy-sell whole life |
| High income, maxed qualified plans | IUL for tax-free supplement (move to IUL pipeline) |
| Estate planning need | Whole life for liquidity at death |
