The skill backbone

Every area of the business has repeat tasks. A repeat task becomes a skill. A reliable skill becomes an automation. This is the map for Full Circle and Full Circle OS.

DOMAINAn area of the business
TASKSomething done again and again
SKILLThe task written down once; Claude runs it
AUTOMATIONA skill that runs on its own trigger

Rules of thumb

When a task earns each step.

Done by hand twiceMake it a skill.
Skill works 5 times in a rowAutomate it on its trigger.
It sends, spends, files or publishesKeep a human approval step, always.
