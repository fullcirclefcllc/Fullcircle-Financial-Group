## Overview
Guide client through trust and estate planning needs. FCFG facilitates the conversation and coordinates with an estate planning attorney for document drafting.

> Note: Trust documents must be drafted by a licensed estate planning attorney. FCFG's role is to identify need, educate, and refer to trusted counsel.

## Step 1 — Identify Estate Planning Needs
Ask during intake / financial snapshot:
- [ ] Do you have a will?
- [ ] Do you have a living trust?
- [ ] Who are your beneficiaries on all accounts and policies?
- [ ] Do you have minor children?
- [ ] Do you own real estate?
- [ ] Do you have business interests to pass on?
- [ ] Have you thought about what happens if you're incapacitated (not just deceased)?

## Step 2 — Educate on Trust Types

| Trust | Purpose | Who Controls |
|---|---|---|
| Revocable Living Trust | Avoids probate, keeps things private | You (grantor) |
| Irrevocable Trust | Asset protection, Medicaid planning | Trustee (not you) |
| Land Trust | Holds real estate anonymously | Trustee |
| Testamentary Trust | Created via will, activates at death | Trustee |

## Step 3 — Beneficiary Audit
Review and align beneficiaries across:
- Life insurance policies
- IUL policies
- Retirement accounts (IRA, 401k)
- Bank accounts (POD — Payable on Death)
- Business interests (operating agreement succession clause)

## Step 4 — Refer to Estate Attorney
- Provide client with referral to trusted estate planning attorney
- Coordinate if needed for trust-owned LLC structure
