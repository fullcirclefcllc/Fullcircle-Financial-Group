# Tax Strategy — Research

## Key Strategies for High-Income 1099 / Business Owners

### Self-Employment Tax Reduction
- **S-Corp Election on LLC** — pay yourself a reasonable salary; remaining profit passes as distribution (not subject to SE tax of 15.3%)
- **Threshold:** Generally worth it at $40K+ net profit

### Deductions (Business)
| Category | Examples |
|---|---|
| Home Office | Dedicated space — % of rent/mortgage, utilities |
| Vehicle | Mileage (67¢/mile for 2024) or actual expenses |
| Health Insurance | 100% deductible for self-employed |
| Retirement Contributions | SEP-IRA (up to 25% of net), Solo 401(k) |
| Education & Training | Courses, books, coaching related to business |
| Technology | Software, equipment, phone (business %) |
| Professional Services | CPA, attorney, financial advisor |
| Marketing | Ads, website, design, printing |

### IUL as a Tax Strategy
- Premiums paid with after-tax dollars
- Cash value grows tax-deferred
- Policy loans accessed income-tax-free
- Death benefit passes income-tax-free
- Not counted as an asset for financial aid (FAFSA)

### Retirement Vehicles

| Vehicle | 2024 Contribution Limit | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| SEP-IRA | Up to $69,000 (25% of net) | Easy to set up, flexible |
| Solo 401(k) | $23,000 + $46,000 employer = $69,000 | Best for high earners |
| Roth IRA | $7,000 ($8,000 if 50+) | Income limits apply |
