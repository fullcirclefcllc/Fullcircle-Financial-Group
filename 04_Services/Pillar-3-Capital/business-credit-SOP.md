## Overview
Build a business credit profile from scratch using a tiered approach — from foundation to revolving credit — without touching the client's personal credit.

## Pre-Requisites (Foundation — Week 1–2)

- [ ] LLC formed and active
- [ ] EIN obtained
- [ ] Business bank account open (30+ days preferred)
- [ ] Business phone number (not a cell — Google Voice or VoIP acceptable)
- [ ] Business address (not home address)
- [ ] Business email (professional domain preferred)
- [ ] D-U-N-S number registered at dnb.com (free)
- [ ] Business listed in D&B database

**Do not apply for credit until all foundation items are complete.**

## Tier 1 — Net-30 Vendor Accounts (Month 1–3)

Target: 3–5 accounts reporting to D&B, Experian Business, or Equifax Business.

See: `03_Research/Business-Credit/README.md` for vendor list.

- Apply with business name, EIN, business address
- Make small purchases ($50–$200) and pay NET-30 (on time or early)
- Confirm each account is reporting to bureaus

**Milestone:** PAYDEX score of 80+ (pay on time) or 90–100 (pay early)

## Tier 2 — Store / Revolving Accounts (Month 3–6)

After Tier 1 accounts are established and reporting:
- Apply for store cards (Home Depot Business, Staples, Sam's Club Business)
- Target: $2K–$10K limits
- No personal guarantee required at this stage if profile is established

## Tier 3 — Bank Credit Cards & Lines (Month 6–12)

After 6+ months of reporting history:
- Apply for business credit cards (Chase Ink, Amex Business, etc.)
- Apply for business lines of credit (SBA, bank, credit union)
- Business credit score and payment history are primary factors

## Monitoring

| Bureau | How to Monitor | Frequency |
|---|---|---|
| D&B | nav.com or creditsafe.com | Monthly |
| Experian Business | nav.com | Monthly |
| Equifax Business | nav.com | Monthly |

## Client Communication Checkpoints

- Week 2: Foundation confirmed, D-U-N-S submitted
- Month 1: Tier 1 accounts applied and first purchases made
- Month 3: Confirm all accounts reporting, review PAYDEX
- Month 6: Tier 2 assessment, apply if ready
- Month 12: Full credit profile review and Tier 3 opportunities
