## Overview

This proposal outlines a formal strategic partnership between FullCircle Financial Group and Rize Mortgage — two brands that serve the same high-income professionals from complementary angles.

Like Rize's collaborations with Ethos and Right Way Mortgage, this partnership is built on deliberate alignment — both firms serving realtors, loan officers, and entrepreneurs, with no service overlap and natural referral flow between them.

## Why This Partnership Works

| Rize Mortgage | FullCircle Financial Group |
|---|---|
| Mortgage origination, lending | Entity structure, protection, credit, IUL, tax strategy |
| Clients need funding to buy | Clients need the system to keep what they earn |
| Pre-transaction relationship | Post-transaction optimization |
| Builds the asset | Protects and leverages the asset |

**The gap we close together:** A realtor who closes with Rize gets into a home — but without an LLC, proper protection, and business credit, they're exposed. FullCircle closes that gap. And a FullCircle client building business credit for real estate needs a mortgage partner they can trust. Rize fills that role.

## Eight Collaboration Pillars

### Pillar 01 — Client Referral & Service Integration

**The play:** Formal two-way referral pipeline with defined economics.

- Rize refers clients who need entity structure, credit repair, protection, or tax strategy → FullCircle
- FullCircle refers clients who need mortgage, refinancing, or lending → Rize
- Co-branded intake form for shared clients streamlines the handoff
- Referral economics: 10–15% of first engagement fee, or negotiated flat fee per qualified referral

**Why it works:** Every top-producing realtor is simultaneously a potential mortgage borrower, a potential credit repair client, and a potential entity formation client. You're already talking to all of them.

### Pillar 02 — Co-Hosted Events & Speaking Platform

**The play:** Productize what worked at the May event. Make it quarterly.

- Quarterly "Wealth Architecture" education events — co-hosted, co-marketed
- Rotating format: Rize presents on mortgage strategy; FullCircle presents on entity/tax/protection
- Audience: shared realtor/LO network plus each brand's individual lists
- Lead capture at every event: QR code to Calendly + landing page

**Format options:**
- 90-minute workshop at a brokerage (free, fills pipeline)
- Half-day paid summit for top producers ($97–$197, self-liquidating)
- Virtual webinar series (evergreen content for both brands)

### Pillar 03 — Content & Authority Building

**The play:** Joint content that positions both brands as the definitive resource for realtor/LO financial education.

- Co-authored guide: "The Top Producer's Complete Financial Playbook" (PDF lead magnet)
- Monthly co-branded newsletter segment
- Cross-promoted social content: Rize promotes FullCircle's audit offer; FullCircle promotes Rize's rate alerts
- Email list swaps: curated introduction to each other's subscribers

### Pillar 04 — Done-With-You Partner Program

**The scale play:** Certify Rize's top producers on the FullCircle methodology.

- Co-develop a "Certified Financial Architect Partner" program for realtors and LOs
- Rize's top agents learn the 4-pillar framework, deliver basic financial health workshops to their clients
- FullCircle handles all complex implementation
- Rize agents get a competitive differentiator; FullCircle gets warm referrals with pre-educated prospects

### Pillar 05 — Joint Mastermind & Retreat Series

**The premium play:** Annual invite-only retreat for top-tier producers.

- High-price, low-volume: $5,000–$10,000/seat
- Rize and FullCircle co-host, sponsor speakers, set the agenda
- Builds a private community of high producers anchored to both brands
- Target: 20 attendees year 1 → $100K–$200K event revenue + downstream client flow

### Pillar 06 — Co-Branded Media Property (Podcast)

**The long-game authority asset:**

- Weekly podcast: "The Wealth Architect" or similar
- Format: Breyon and AJ co-host, rotate guests from shared network
- Topics: mortgage strategy, entity structure, credit, IUL, tax, real estate investing
- Published on YouTube, Spotify, Apple Podcasts
- Clips repurposed across both brands' social channels
- Becomes a permanent lead-gen asset that compounds for years

### Pillar 07 — Shared Client Experience

**The differentiation play:**

- Co-branded welcome sequence for clients referred between firms
- Coordinated onboarding so clients feel one unified experience, not two handoffs
- Shared client milestones: Rize notifies FullCircle when a client closes → FullCircle starts entity formation
- Optional: shared client portal (GoHighLevel or white-label CRM)

### Pillar 08 — Community & Charitable Initiative

**The brand equity play:**

- Joint financial literacy initiative for first-time homebuyers or underserved communities
- Both brands sponsor/co-host free financial education workshops
- Earns media coverage, builds community standing, gives partner agents a story to share
- Aligns with Breyon's mission to close the wealth gap for communities historically excluded from these systems
