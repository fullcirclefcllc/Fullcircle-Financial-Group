# Client Pipeline Flow
## FullCircle Financial Group — Full CRM System

---

## Pipeline Overview

```
AWARENESS → LEAD → PRE-QUALIFIED → DISCOVERY → PROPOSED → DECISION → ENROLLED
    → ONBOARDING → ACTIVE → MILESTONE REVIEW → RETAINED → REFERRAL ADVOCATE
```

Every contact lives in exactly one stage. Move them forward deliberately. Never let a contact sit without a scheduled next action.

---

## Stage Definitions & Actions

---

### STAGE 1 — AWARENESS
*They've seen your content, been referred, or encountered your brand. They don't know you personally yet.*

**How they get here:**
- Social media (Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, YouTube)
- Referral partner sends them your name
- They attend a team presentation
- They see a post shared by a mutual connection

**Your action:**
- None required yet — this is passive
- Track referral partners who are generating awareness
- Monitor which content generates the most inbound messages

**Move to Lead when:** They follow, comment, DM, or a referral partner gives you their contact info

---

### STAGE 2 — LEAD
*You have their contact info or they've expressed interest. First contact not yet made.*

**How they get here:**
- They DM you asking about services
- A referral partner provides their name and number
- They complete a contact form or opt-in
- They comment "interested" on a post

**Your action (within 24 hours):**
- Send a personal message: "Hey [NAME] — thanks for reaching out. I'd love to learn more about your situation. Can I send you a quick 5-minute survey to review before we talk? It helps me come prepared."
- Send pre-intake survey link
- Add to pipeline tracker with source, date, and notes

**Move to Pre-Qualified when:** They complete the pre-intake survey

**If no response in 48 hours:** Follow up once more — different channel

---

### STAGE 3 — PRE-QUALIFIED
*Pre-intake survey completed. You've reviewed their answers. Call is scheduled.*

**Your action:**
- Review pre-intake survey before the call
- Score them using the Ideal Client Scoring Matrix (`06_Brand/ideal-client-avatar.md`)
- Identify top 2–3 pain points and goals to address on the call
- Confirm call appointment 24 hours before
- Prepare a 3-bullet preview of what their blueprint might look like

**Move to Discovery when:** Discovery call is held

**Red flags to watch for:**
- Score below 8 on the client scoring matrix → nurture only, don't rush to close
- "I'm just researching" answer on decision clarity question → set expectations, longer nurture
- Income under $75K → may be better fit for a starter package

---

### STAGE 4 — DISCOVERY CALL
*30-minute strategy call. Your job: diagnose, educate, recommend.*

**Call Structure:**
| Time | What Happens |
|---|---|
| 0–5 min | Build rapport. "Tell me what's going on with your income situation right now." |
| 5–15 min | Reveal the 3 gaps (taxes, protection, capital). Ask which resonates. Listen. |
| 15–23 min | Walk through their custom blueprint recommendation. Make it specific to them. |
| 23–28 min | Tie their goal to the solution. "If we solve [X], you get [Y] result." |
| 28–30 min | Close: "Based on everything you told me, [Pathway X] is the right fit. The investment is $[AMOUNT]. Are you ready to get started?" |

**If they say yes on the call:**
- "Perfect. I'll send the agreement and deposit link within the hour. Once I receive that, we schedule your onboarding call within 48 hours."

**If they say they need to think:**
- "Totally understand. What specific question or concern would I need to answer for you to feel confident moving forward?" → address it
- "When can we reconnect — Thursday or Friday?" → always get a specific time

**Your action after the call:**
- Send proposal (same day — within 2 hours of call)
- Send follow-up text: "Great talking with you. Proposal is in your inbox. Text me if you have any questions."

**Move to Proposed when:** Proposal is sent

---

### STAGE 5 — PROPOSED
*Proposal delivered. Awaiting decision. Clock is ticking.*

**Your action:**
- Day 1 (same day): Send proposal + text confirmation
- Day 2: Check-in text — "Hey [NAME] — just want to make sure you got the proposal. Any questions I can answer?"
- Day 4: Follow-up call — "I wanted to hop on a quick call to answer any questions before you make a decision."
- Day 7: Final follow-up — "I have a spot open right now and want to hold it for you. Let me know if you're moving forward or if the timing isn't right — either is fine."

**If 7 days pass with no response:** Move to a 30-day nurture sequence (monthly check-in, educational content)

**Move to Decision when:** They respond with questions, objections, or a "yes"

---

### STAGE 6 — DECISION
*They're engaged but haven't signed yet. Handle objections. Close.*

**Common objections and responses:** See individual service marketing files  
**Close techniques:**
- The specificity close: "If we start this week, your LLC can be active in 10–14 days. That means you're structured for the full quarter."
- The urgency close: "Tax strategy works best when we implement early in the year — the window to capture Q2 deductions is now."
- The ROI close: "My fee for the Foundation Blueprint is $4,500. The average client saves $10,000–$25,000 in year one. The math works in your favor."

**Move to Enrolled when:** Agreement signed and deposit collected

---

### STAGE 7 — ENROLLED
*Signed agreement + deposit received. They are a client.*

**Your action (same day):**
- Send welcome message: "Welcome to FullCircle — you made the right decision. Here's what happens next."
- Schedule onboarding call within 48 hours
- Create client folder: `02_Clients/[LastName-FirstName]/`
- Copy all templates into their folder
- Send full intake form (if not already completed in pre-intake survey)

**Move to Onboarding when:** Onboarding call is scheduled

---

### STAGE 8 — ONBOARDING
*First working call. Set the blueprint, start delivery.*

**Onboarding Call Agenda (60 min):**
| Time | Action |
|---|---|
| 0–15 min | Complete financial snapshot together |
| 15–30 min | Present their custom entity diagram (before vs. after) |
| 30–45 min | Set 30/60/90 day milestones in progress tracker |
| 45–55 min | Begin Pillar 1 immediately (collect info for LLC filing) |
| 55–60 min | Set expectations: "You'll hear from me at every milestone. Here's how to reach me." |

**Your action after onboarding:**
- Begin LLC filing (same week)
- Register D-U-N-S (submit same day)
- Order IUL illustration from carrier/BGA (if applicable)
- Update progress tracker

**Move to Active when:** At least one deliverable is in motion

---

### STAGE 9 — ACTIVE CLIENT
*Delivery in progress. Consistent milestone communication required.*

**Weekly internal checklist (per active client):**
- [ ] What is their current milestone?
- [ ] What is blocking progress?
- [ ] When did I last communicate with them?
- [ ] What's the next scheduled touchpoint?

**Communication cadence:**
- Week 1: LLC filing status update
- Week 2: EIN confirmation, business bank account guidance
- Week 3: D-U-N-S confirmation, Tier 1 vendor list sent
- Week 4: IUL application status, 30-day check-in call

**Move to Milestone Review when:** 30-day mark is reached

---

### STAGE 10 — MILESTONE REVIEW
*Structured check-in calls at 30, 90, 180, and 365 days.*

**Review Call Structure (30 min):**
1. What milestones have been completed?
2. What results has the client experienced?
3. What's next — what pillar do we focus on?
4. Are there additional services to layer in?
5. Referral ask: "Who else do you know that needs this?"

**Use:** Post-service survey (`02_Clients/_templates/post-service-survey.md`)

**Move to Retained when:** Client signs annual advisory retainer

---

### STAGE 11 — RETAINED (Annual Advisory Client)
*Client is on a recurring retainer. Ongoing relationship.*

**Retainer Investment:** $2,400–$5,000/year  
**Includes:**
- 4 quarterly strategy calls
- Annual financial review
- Tax strategy coordination (with in-house CPA)
- Priority access to Breyon
- Annual beneficiary and estate plan audit
- Business credit monitoring and tier advancement

**Touchpoints:**
- Q1: Annual review + tax strategy call
- Q2: Business credit check + IUL annual report review
- Q3: Mid-year planning — any life changes, new opportunities
- Q4: Year-end tax planning — final decisions before December 31

---

### STAGE 12 — REFERRAL ADVOCATE
*They've referred at least one person. Highest-value client relationship.*

**Your action:**
- Thank personally (call or handwritten note)
- Track referrals in `07_Operations/referral-process.md`
- Provide referral incentive (credit, gift, or reciprocal referral)
- Feature their story (with permission) as a case study or testimonial
- Invite to exclusive events, first access to new services

## Pipeline Metrics to Track Weekly

| Metric | Target |
|---|---|
| New leads added | 10+/week |
| Pre-intake surveys sent | Equal to leads |
| Discovery calls held | 5–8/week |
| Proposals sent | Equal to calls |
| Close rate (calls → enrolled) | 30–50% |
| Average days to close (lead → enrolled) | Under 14 days |
| Active clients | Growing month-over-month |
| Referrals generated | 1+ per 3 clients |
| Retained clients (retainer) | Growing quarterly |
