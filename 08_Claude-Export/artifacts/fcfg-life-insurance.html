    Why you need it

    What life insurance is for

    Income replacement

    If your family depends on your paycheck or your draw from the business, coverage can help replace that income for the years they'd need it.

    Debts and business loans

    Car loans, cards and business loans you personally guarantee don't disappear. Coverage can help pay them so they don't fall on your family.

    Key person

    If the business depends on one person, a policy owned by the business can help it get through the loss of that person, hire, or wind down in order.

    Buy-sell funding

    When partners agree to buy out an owner's share at death, life insurance is a common way to fund that agreement. The agreement itself belongs with your attorney.

    Legacy

    Some people use coverage to leave something behind for children, grandchildren or causes they care about.

    When you need it

    The moments that call for a review

    • FamilyA new family

      Marriage, a new baby or a blended family. More people depend on you, and beneficiaries should be set on purpose.

    • HomeBuying a home

      A mortgage is usually the biggest debt a family carries. Coverage can help your family stay in the home.

    • LoanTaking a business loan

      Some lenders, including some SBA loans, may require life insurance on the owner as a condition of their loan. That's the lender's requirement, not FCFG's.

    • PartnerAdding a partner

      Plan what happens if an owner dies. A buy-sell agreement and the coverage to fund it are worth discussing early.

    • TeamHiring a key employee

      When one person's skills drive revenue, key person coverage is worth a conversation.

    • LegacyPlanning a legacy

      Estate and legacy plans often include life insurance. Coordinate with your attorney and tax professional.

      FAQ

      Questions clients ask

      Do I have to buy insurance to work with FCFG?

      No. Insurance is optional and never a condition of any credit or funding service. You can use any FCFG service without ever discussing insurance.

      Who offers the coverage?

      Life insurance is offered through [Producer name], a licensed Arizona insurance producer, License # [number]. Policies are issued by insurance carriers.

      Will I need a medical exam?

      It depends on the carrier, the policy and the coverage amount. Some policies use health questions and records instead of an exam. Coverage is subject to underwriting and approval.

      What is key person coverage?

      A policy the business owns on someone the business depends on. If that person dies, the benefit goes to the business to help it through the loss.

      What is a buy-sell agreement?

      An agreement between owners about what happens to a share of the business when an owner dies, becomes disabled or leaves. Life insurance is one common way to fund the buyout. The agreement should be drafted by your attorney.

      Are policy illustrations guaranteed?

      No. Policy illustrations are not guarantees; indexed and dividend values can change. Whole life policies include guaranteed values stated in the contract, and we show you which numbers are guaranteed and which aren't.

      I'm an investor with plenty of assets. Why would I need life insurance?

      Assets aren't the same as cash. Properties carry mortgages, partners and costs that don't stop at death. Coverage can give your family or the business cash to pay debts, buy out a partner or keep the properties instead of selling them under pressure.

      Does putting my properties in an LLC make me lawsuit-proof?

      No. An entity can help separate one property's problems from your other assets, but it has to be set up and maintained properly, with separate accounts and records. Liability insurance still matters. Your attorney prepares the documents; we help you plan the structure.

      Can I use a policy's cash value toward my next deal?

      Some permanent policies let you borrow against the cash value. Policy loans accrue interest, reduce the death benefit and, if not managed, can cause the policy to lapse and create taxes. Cash value usually builds slowly in the early years. We show you how it works in writing before you decide.

      Pricing is quoted in writing on your strategy call. Premiums depend on things like age, health, policy type and coverage amount.
