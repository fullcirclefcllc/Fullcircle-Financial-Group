The client journey

Kickoff to Protect, and what you'll hear along the way

Not every client runs every stage. Wherever you start, the pattern is the same: clear next steps, and regular status updates even when we're waiting.

  1. Kickoff

    Strategy call and onboarding

    We look at your personal and business picture, agree on the order, and put the plan and pricing in writing. You get portal access and a document checklist.

    What you'll hear from us

    A written plan, your checklist, and the date of your first status update.

  2. Fix it

    Personal credit

    Three-bureau review together, disputes only on items believed inaccurate, and utilization coaching.

    What you'll hear from us

    Each dispute sent, each bureau response, and the weeks we're still waiting on one.

  3. Build it

    Structure and business credit

    Entity review and clean-up, then D-U-N-S, reporting vendor accounts and monitoring of the business reports.

    What you'll hear from us

    Filings completed, accounts reporting, and changes on your business files.

  4. Fund it

    Funding

    A review of the full profile, matched to lenders that fit. Every product, hard inquiry and fee disclosed in writing first. You choose what goes out.

    What you'll hear from us

    Where each application stands, lender requests, and decisions as they come in.

  5. Protect it

    Life insurance, if you want it

    A needs review and plain-words comparison, through a licensed Arizona producer. Optional, and never a condition of anything else.

    What you'll hear from us

    Underwriting status, then an annual review.

FAQ

Questions clients ask

Where do I start?

Use the "Where are you now?" selector above for a starting point. On your strategy call we look at your personal and business profile and confirm the right first step in writing.

Do I need all four services?

No. Each service stands on its own, and many clients start in the middle. The order is a guide to what supports what.

How do fees work?

Pricing is quoted in writing on your strategy call. The only fee stated here is the funding success fee: 5-10% of the funded amount, capped at $10,000, invoiced only after your funds are received, on the amount you accept.

Personal credit repair is never billed upfront. You pay only as the law allows, after the work is done, as spelled out in your written agreement.

Is life insurance required?

No. Insurance is optional and never a condition of any credit or funding service. It's offered through [Producer name], a licensed Arizona insurance producer.

Do you guarantee results?

No. FCFG does not guarantee any score change, deletion, approval, funding amount or timeline. Results vary.

How will I hear from you?

Status updates come on a regular schedule, through your client portal and by the contact method you choose, including the weeks we're waiting on a bureau or lender.

What's the client portal?

A secure place to upload documents and see where each step stands. Everyone gets the same onboarding and the same portal.

Can I dispute credit report errors myself?

Yes. You can dispute inaccurate information on your credit report yourself, for free, directly with the bureaus.
