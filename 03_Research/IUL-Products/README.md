## Key Evaluation Criteria

- **Cap Rate** — maximum credited interest in a given period
- **Participation Rate** — % of index gain credited to policy
- **Floor** — minimum credited rate (typically 0%, protecting against loss)
- **Loan Provisions** — fixed vs. variable; participating vs. non-participating
- **Surrender Charges** — schedule and duration
- **Carrier Rating** — A.M. Best, Moody's, S&P rating
