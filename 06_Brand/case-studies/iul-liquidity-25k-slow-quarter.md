# Case Study — IUL Liquidity

**Client type:** High-income producer, variable income  
**Timeline:** 5 years (policy in force)  
**Product:** Indexed Universal Life

---

## The Problem

Client had strong income but zero liquid reserves outside taxable accounts. During a slow quarter, they withdrew $25K from personal savings — triggering stress, missed opportunities, and no tax-efficient backup plan.

---

## The System We Built

| Element | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| Product | IUL — $750/mo premium |
| Death benefit | $1,500,000 |
| Primary goal | Tax-free liquidity during income dips |
| Structure | Funded through OpCo entity (business purpose) |

---

## The Outcome

| Event | Old Path | New Path |
|-------|----------|----------|
| Slow quarter cash need | Personal savings ($25K withdrawn) | **$25K policy loan — tax-free, no bank** |
| Tax impact | N/A (already taxed savings) | No taxable event |
| Death benefit | Group term only | $1.5M permanent + CSV growth |
| Retirement supplement | 401(k) maxed only | IUL CSV as tax-free income stream |
