# SOP — IUL Consultation & Application

**Pillar:** 3 — Capital  
**Owner:** Breyon Miller  
**Version:** 1.0

---

## Overview
Walk client through IUL needs analysis, carrier selection, illustration review, and application submission.

## Step 1 — Needs Analysis

Gather from financial snapshot:
- Age and health status (underwriting impact)
- Income / ability to fund premium
- Desired death benefit
- Primary goal: protection, retirement supplement, or liquidity vehicle
- Existing life insurance policies

**Key qualifying questions:**
- How much can you commit monthly? (Minimum $300–$500/mo for meaningful cash value build)
- What is your health history?
- Do you smoke?
- How soon do you need access to cash value? (3–5 year horizon for meaningful CSV)

## Step 2 — Carrier Selection

See: `03_Research/IUL-Products/README.md` for carrier tracker.

Select based on:
- Client health profile → underwriting tier
- Premium budget → minimum face amount
- Primary objective (CSV growth vs. death benefit vs. balanced)
- Cap rates and participation rates

## Step 3 — Run Illustration

Request illustration from carrier or BGA showing:
- Assumed premium
- Death benefit
- Projected cash value at years 5, 10, 15, 20
- Projected tax-free loan value
- Guaranteed vs. non-guaranteed columns

Review illustration with client — focus on:
- Year they can access meaningful CSV
- What a policy loan looks like in practice
- What happens if they miss a premium

## Step 4 — Application

- [ ] Complete application (carrier form + e-app if available)
- [ ] HIPAA authorization signed
- [ ] Beneficiary designation completed
- [ ] Owner / insured / beneficiary clearly designated
- [ ] Submit to carrier / BGA

## Step 5 — Underwriting

- Paramed exam scheduled (if required by face amount / carrier)
- Medical records requested (if needed)
- Underwriting decision: Preferred Plus → Standard → Rated → Declined

## Step 6 — Policy Issue & Delivery

- [ ] Policy issued — review with client
- [ ] Confirm premium payment method set up
- [ ] Explain policy loan process
- [ ] Set annual review date
- [ ] Log in client progress tracker
