## 🔴 BLOCKING GAPS — Fix These First

### 1. No Pricing Defined
**Gap:** All fee fields were blank. You cannot quote, close, or collect without defined prices.  
**Fix:** Pricing now set in `07_Operations/pricing-and-packages.md`  
**Revenue impact:** Every day without pricing = zero closes possible.

### 2. No Proposal Template
**Gap:** `05_Templates-and-Proposals/proposals/` was empty. After a discovery call, there's nothing to send the prospect.  
**Fix:** Proposal template created at `05_Templates-and-Proposals/proposals/proposal-template.md`  
**Revenue impact:** Prospects go cold when there's no follow-up document within 24 hours of a call.

### 3. No Lead Pipeline Tracker
**Gap:** No visibility into who is a lead, who is a warm prospect, who has been quoted, who stalled.  
**Fix:** Pipeline tracker created at `07_Operations/lead-pipeline.md`  
**Revenue impact:** Without a pipeline, you're guessing at your revenue. Leads fall through the cracks.

### 4. No Urgency Mechanism in the Sales Conversation
**Gap:** The onboarding workflow starts at "inquiry received" — there is no structured reason for a prospect to act *now* vs. "I'll think about it."  
**Fix:** Use legislation hook (QBI deduction permanence, tax season timing) + deposit incentive ($750 strategy session applies toward package) as urgency triggers.  
**Revenue impact:** Without urgency, close timelines stretch to 30–60+ days.

### 5. No Outbound Outreach System
**Gap:** The entire process is reactive (waits for inquiries). There is no proactive outbound to warm leads, past contacts, or referral partners.  
**Fix:** 14-day sprint plan includes specific outbound actions for every day.  
**Revenue impact:** In a 2-week sprint, inbound alone will not hit $50K. Outbound is mandatory.

## 🟡 HURTING GAPS — Fix in Week 2

### 6. Referral Partners Not Activated
**Gap:** `07_Operations/vendor-partner-list.md` has no partners listed. Real estate brokers, mortgage brokers, and CPAs are the highest-leverage referral sources — none are currently engaged.  
**Fix:** Identify 3–5 warm contacts who run teams or have networks. Request to do a 20-minute team presentation.  
**Revenue impact:** One team presentation to 15 agents can generate 2–4 clients ($9K–$18K from a single conversation).

### 7. No Follow-Up Sequence After Discovery Calls
**Gap:** No defined follow-up cadence after a call. If someone says "let me think about it," the system ends there.  
**Fix:** Add a 3-touch follow-up sequence (Day 1 email, Day 3 check-in, Day 7 value-add) to the onboarding workflow.  
**Revenue impact:** Industry standard: 80% of closes happen after 5+ follow-up touches. Most advisors stop at 1–2.

### 8. IUL Not Used as a Lead Magnet
**Gap:** IUL consultation is listed as $500, but the real revenue is the carrier commission. Zero-cost IUL consultations are a powerful way to get a client into the relationship — then upsell structure and credit.  
**Fix:** Consider making IUL consultations free (commission-compensated) and use them as the entry point for the full blueprint pitch.  
**Revenue impact:** Lowers the barrier to first contact; creates a relationship before asking for $4.5K–$12.5K.

### 9. No Social Proof / Case Study Formatted for Sharing
**Gap:** The presentations mention client results ("$40K in taxes saved," "$120K business credit in 90 days") but there are no formatted case studies ready to share on social media or in conversations.  
**Fix:** Create 2–3 one-page case study cards for the most powerful outcomes.  
**Revenue impact:** A shareable result story posted on Instagram or LinkedIn can generate 5–15 inbound inquiries organically.

### 10. Service Agreement Has No E-Signature Path
**Gap:** The service agreement is a markdown template — there's no way to collect a signature digitally, which slows the close.  
**Fix:** Convert to DocuSign, HelloSign, or use a PDF form that can be signed on mobile. The faster you can get a signature, the faster the money moves.  
**Revenue impact:** Every extra step between "yes" and signed agreement = churn risk.
