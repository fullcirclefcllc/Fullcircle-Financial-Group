**Minimum closes needed:** 4 × Full Architecture ($12,500) OR 11 × Foundation Blueprint ($4,500)

---

## The Only 3 Things That Drive Revenue This Sprint

1. **Conversations** — You cannot close someone you haven't talked to. Volume of conversations = volume of closes.
2. **Follow-up** — Most closes happen on follow-up #3–5. Every open conversation needs a scheduled next touch.
3. **Speed to proposal** — Send the proposal the same day as the discovery call. Same day. Not tomorrow.

## Content Hooks That Drive Inbound (Post 1–2x/week)

**Post 1 — Tax Hook**
> "If you made over $100K last year and you're still filing as a sole proprietor, you paid thousands more in taxes than you needed to. Here's what a proper structure looks like. [Diagram or 3-bullet breakdown]"

**Post 2 — Protection Hook**
> "One lawsuit. One unhappy client. One car accident. If your name is on everything — your business, your home, your savings — one judgment can take all of it. This is why structure matters before the lawsuit, not after."

**Post 3 — Liquidity Hook**
> "Market slows. Commission dries up. No deals for 60 days. Most agents have zero backup. Here's what the ones who survive (and thrive) have that you don't: an IUL with cash value they can pull from in 5 business days, tax-free, no credit check."

**Post 4 — Result Story**
> "A client came to me paying $40K in taxes with nothing to show for it. 60 days later: LLC formed. IUL funded. Trust in place. First year savings covered my fee 3x over. This is what a system looks like."

---

## Urgency Hooks to Use in Every Conversation

1. **Tax legislation:** "The QBI deduction is being made permanent — if you're not structured to capture it, you're leaving money on the table right now."
2. **Tax season setup:** "The best time to structure for next tax year is Q2. If we start now, your entity is active for the full fiscal year."
3. **Spot scarcity:** "I take on a limited number of new clients each month so I can deliver at the level my clients expect. I have [X] spots open right now."
4. **Speed of results:** "Most clients have their LLC active and EIN in hand within 2 weeks of starting. The sooner we begin, the sooner that's true for you."
