## The Core Message

**IUL is not life insurance. It's a financial system that happens to include a death benefit.**

Most people think of life insurance as something that pays out when you die. IUL does that — but while you're alive, it builds a cash reserve you can access tax-free, grows with the market without losing in a crash, and acts as your own private bank when you need capital fast.
