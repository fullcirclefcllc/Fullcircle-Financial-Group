## Overview
Step-by-step process for forming an LLC for a client, from intake through first bank account.

---

## Pre-Formation Checklist

- [ ] Client intake complete
- [ ] Determine state of formation (home state vs. Wyoming / Nevada for privacy)
- [ ] Determine entity type: Single-Member LLC, Multi-Member LLC, or PLLC
- [ ] Decide if holding company is needed alongside operating LLC
- [ ] Choose registered agent (client's or third-party)

## Step 1 — Name the Entity
- Search state SOS database for name availability
- Confirm name does not infringe on trademarks (USPTO.gov)
- Suggested format: `[Name/Brand] LLC` or `[Holding Co] Holdings LLC`

## Step 2 — File Articles of Organization
- File with Secretary of State in chosen state
- Typical cost: $50–$150
- Turnaround: Same day (expedited) to 2–4 weeks standard
- Collect: Stamped / filed Articles of Organization

## Step 3 — Obtain EIN
- File IRS Form SS-4 (online at IRS.gov — free, instant)
- Use LLC name and client's SSN as responsible party
- Save EIN confirmation letter (147C or CP 575)

## Step 4 — Draft Operating Agreement
- Use template in `05_Templates-and-Proposals/`
- Key sections: member(s), ownership %, management structure, profit distribution, dissolution
- Sign and date; store in `01_Corporate-Governance/Formation/`

## Step 5 — Business Address
- Do NOT use client's home address (privacy risk)
- Options: Virtual office, UPS Store suite, registered agent address, co-working space

## Step 6 — Open Business Bank Account
- Requirements: Articles of Organization, EIN letter, Operating Agreement, ID
- Recommended: Credit unions or community banks for relationship building
- Separate account = separate entity (essential for liability protection)

## Step 7 — Register with D&B
- Go to dnb.com → get free D-U-N-S number
- Allow 30 days before applying for credit
- Log in client tracker under Pillar 3 — Business Credit

## Step 8 — Update Client Progress Tracker
- Mark all LLC milestones complete in `02_Clients/[ClientName]/progress-tracker.md`
