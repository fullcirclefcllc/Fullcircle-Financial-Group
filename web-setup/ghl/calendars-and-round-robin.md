---

## Round-Robin Rules

| Calendar | Assignment |
|----------|------------|
| Term | Round-robin among users with `agent_cert_term=true` AND `status:active-agent` AND license in client state |
| Whole | `agent_cert_whole` |
| IUL | `agent_cert_iul` only — never dump IUL leads on Term-only agents |
| Agency Demo | Fixed: agency owner or FullCircle closer |
| Overflow | If no one available in 24h → queue to Sales Manager + SMS alert |

---

## Reminders (all public calendars)

- Email confirm immediately  
- SMS 24 hours before  
- SMS 1 hour before  
- No-show → No-Show Recovery workflow  

Buffers: 10 minutes after IUL reviews. Max 6 discovery calls/day per producer (quality over spray).
