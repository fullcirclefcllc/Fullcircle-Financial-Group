Pillar One

Structure & Protection: LLC vs PLLC

✓ Traditional LLC

Recommended for Realtors, Loan Officers & Entrepreneurs

  • Shields personal assets from liability
  • Reduces self-employment tax (15.3% down significantly)
  • Builds business credit profile
  • You maintain your professional license
  • Simple to set up and maintain

• Professional LLC (PLLC)

Information Only

  • Requires E&O insurance verification
  • License appears as entity name (varies by state)
  • Higher compliance requirements
  • Most states don't require it for agents
  • Better for brokers, team leads

How It Works in Practice

When you're structured as an LLC, the business is a separate legal entity. Client sues the business for a transaction issue? The lawsuit targets the LLC, not you personally. Your home, your car, your savings—protected.
