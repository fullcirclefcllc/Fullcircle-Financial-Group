Barrett Benefits is a voluntary program offered by Full Circle Financial Group; Barrett does not sponsor, fund or endorse any plan, and all coverage is chosen and paid for by the individual. Supplemental benefits are underwritten by Combined Insurance Company of America, a Chubb company. Simplified issue approval depends on your answers to the health questions. Guaranteed issue applies to eligible group enrollments during the enrollment window, subject to plan limits and age bands. Individual life insurance is underwritten. Dental, vision and telehealth memberships are discount programs, not insurance. Availability and rates vary by state.
