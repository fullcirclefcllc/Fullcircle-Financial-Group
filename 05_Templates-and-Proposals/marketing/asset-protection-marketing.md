# Marketing Material — Asset Protection
## FullCircle Financial Group

---

## The Core Message

**You've worked for 10, 20 years to build what you have. One lawsuit, one disgruntled client, one business dispute can erase it in a courtroom — if everything is in your name.**

Asset protection is not paranoia. It is the standard operating procedure of every wealthy person and business owner who has stayed wealthy. The question is not IF you need it — it is whether you have it before you need it.

---

## Hook Library

**Hook 1 — The Name Search**
> "In the time it takes to search your name on Google, a plaintiff's attorney can find your home address, your vehicles, your business registration, and every asset tied to your name. If you're a real estate agent, a contractor, a healthcare professional — you are one client dispute away from having all of that at risk. This is why the first thing I do for every client is get their name off everything that matters."

**Hook 2 — The Lawsuit Reality**
> "The US files more civil lawsuits per capita than almost any country on earth. Millions of suits are filed every year. Not all of them are frivolous. If your business is in your name, your home is in your name, and your savings are in your name — you are one judgment away from losing all three. I've seen it happen. In 30 years, I've sat across the table from people who thought it couldn't happen to them. Structure protects before the lawsuit — not after."

**Hook 3 — The Invisible Wealthy**
> "Have you ever noticed that truly wealthy people are hard to find online? Their name isn't attached to their real estate. Their business address isn't their home. Their LLC is in Wyoming, registered to a third party. This is not an accident. Privacy IS protection. The less a plaintiff can find, the less they can take."

**Hook 4 — Real Estate Agent Specific**
> "You show homes. You negotiate deals. You represent buyers and sellers in the most important transactions of their lives. If something goes wrong — even if it's not your fault — your client can sue you personally. If your business is in your name, the lawsuit reaches your home, your car, your personal savings. An LLC and the right structure changes that. One entity absorbs the lawsuit. Your personal assets never come into play."

## Objection Handlers

| Objection | Response |
|---|---|
| "I have insurance for that" | "Insurance covers some things. A plaintiff's attorney can still go after personal assets beyond your policy limit — or for things your policy doesn't cover. Structure and insurance work together, not instead of each other." |
| "I've never been sued" | "Neither has anyone — until they are. The structure works because it exists before the threat, not after. After is too late." |
| "Isn't this only for rich people?" | "The people who need this most are the ones building wealth right now — not the ones who already have it locked up. If you have income, a home, savings, or a business — you have something worth protecting." |
| "This sounds like a lot to maintain" | "Once the structure is in place, it's self-maintaining. You file an annual report, keep the accounts separate, and we handle the rest at your annual review." |
