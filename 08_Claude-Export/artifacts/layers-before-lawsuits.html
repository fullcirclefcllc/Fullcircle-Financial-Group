01 — Findings

The Verdict

Two constraints govern this entire division, and neither one is competitive. One is who may say what. The other is when the work was done.

Controlling constraintYou cannot sell the documents. You can own everything around them.

In every state except Arizona, a non-lawyer may not draft entity or trust documents, may not select which structure a specific client should use, may not explain what a document legally does, and may not share a legal fee. The Florida Supreme Court's formulation is the cleanest: "the assembly, drafting, execution and funding of living trust documents constitute the practice of law" — with one carve-out that matters enormously, in the same opinion: "the mere gathering of necessary information … does not constitute the practice of law."

That is not a wall around the business. It is a wall through the middle of it — and everything on your side of that wall is genuinely valuable and almost universally neglected: exposure mapping, insurance-first analysis, timing screens, counsel-ready intake packets, and entity maintenance. Maintenance is the highest-leverage product in this division, because failure to maintain is what actually collapses structures in court, it is unambiguously not legal advice, and it is recurring revenue.

And there is a fact specific to you that changes the strategy. Arizona is the only state in the country that permits non-lawyer ownership of a legal-services entity, through Alternative Business Structure certification by the Arizona Supreme Court — and Arizona permits fee-sharing and referral fees among ABS participants. FCFG is an Arizona firm. That is not a footnote; it is the single largest structural advantage available to this division, and it should be investigated with counsel before you design anything else.

What you can do, in every state

General legal education with no individualized recommendation. Intake and fact-gathering. Project coordination and paralegal work under a lawyer's actual supervision. Ministerial filing and registered agent services. Funding logistics executed on an attorney's instruction. Insurance and financial products under proper licenses. Post-signing maintenance, custody, reminders, and entity bookkeeping.

What is UPL almost everywhere

Drafting or assembling operating agreements, trusts, deeds or bylaws. Recommending "you need a Wyoming holding company" or "an irrevocable trust" to a specific client. Explaining what a charging order or spendthrift clause does for their situation. Applying law to facts. Quasi-legal titles — "certified trust advisor," "estate planning specialist," anything with "legal" in the trade name.

The second constraint is timing, and it is absolute. Under the Uniform Voidable Transactions Act, a transfer made with actual intent to hinder, delay or defraud a creditor can be unwound — proven circumstantially through badges of fraud including transfer to an insider, retained control, concealment, and transfer shortly after a suit or threat. In bankruptcy, 11 U.S.C. §548(e) reaches back ten years for transfers into a self-settled trust. Any engagement that begins after a claim exists, is threatened, or is reasonably foreseeable is not asset protection work — it is a fact pattern. Build a hard stop into intake, not a disclaimer at the bottom of a page.

02 — Structure

System Architecture

Four layers, and a wall running through the middle of the second one. Knowing exactly which side of that wall each activity sits on is the whole operating discipline.

LayerThe question it answersOwnsWho may perform it
DiagnosisWhat does this client actually own and what is actually exposed?Asset and entity inventory, insurance review, liability sources, timing screen, counsel packetYou — fact-gathering and education
DesignWhich structures should this client use, and what will they do?Structure selection, drafting, legal-effect explanation, titling decisions, tax and creditor analysisAttorney only — outside Arizona ABS
ExecutionIs it actually in place?Filings, funding logistics on counsel's instruction, banking setup, insurance placement under licence, recording trackingShared — you execute, counsel directs
MaintenanceWill it hold up in three years?Separate accounts, resolutions, annual filings, capitalization, arm's-length intercompany documentation, annual review cadenceYou — and this is the recurring product

The design rule that keeps you on the right side of the wall

Hard-code a legal-decision gate: any output that selects a structure, states a legal effect, or applies law to facts must be attorney-authored or attorney-approved before it reaches the client — with the approving attorney and timestamp logged. Keep the education library and the advice library in physically separate, separately reviewable stores. Enforce a "no attorney contact, no execution" block. And never let the compensation logic reference legal fees in either direction.

Three viable architectures — pick one deliberately

(1) Arizona ABS. Non-lawyer equity, in-house lawyers, fee-sharing lawful in Arizona. Cleanest economics; requires a compliance lawyer, governance policies, Supreme Court certification and annual renewal — and note that Texas prohibits attorneys from joining firms with non-attorney ownership, and California restricts fee-sharing with out-of-state ABS attorneys, so it does not license nationwide operation. (2) Lawyer-owned firm plus a separate services company paid a fixed, market-rate fee for genuinely non-legal work that does not vary with legal fees or client count, with Rule 5.7 written disclosures on the non-legal side. (3) Pure coordination and product firm — no document selection, no drafting, no legal-effect explanation, clients engage and pay counsel directly, no money moving in either direction. Lowest legal risk, lowest control over quality and timing, which is precisely the operating problem this system has to solve.

05 — Substance

The Layers, In The Order They Actually Matter

Every credible protection process runs in this order. A program that leads with entities before insurance is a marketing program, not a protection program.

LayerWhat it actually doesThe honest limit
0 · InsuranceLiability, umbrella, professional, D&O, EPLI. Cheapest protection per dollar, and the only layer that pays defense costs.None worth naming — this is the layer everyone skips and nobody should.
1 · Statutory exemptionsHomestead, ERISA plans and IRAs, life insurance cash value and annuities, tenancy by the entirety. Free, pre-existing, state-dependent.Wildly variable by state — unlimited homestead in Florida and Texas, as little as $5,000 elsewhere outside bankruptcy.
2 · LLCs and charging ordersInside-out: entity liability does not reach the owner. Outside-in: an owner's personal creditor is limited to a charging order — a lien on distributions with no voting or management rights.Single-member LLCs are the weak form. Olmstead v. FTC (Fla. 2010) held the charging order is not the exclusive remedy against an SMLLC; In re Albright let a bankruptcy trustee liquidate one. A genuine second member with real economic substance is what preserves the argument.
3 · Structural separationHolding company over operating subsidiaries; separate entity per property; Wyoming or Nevada holdco owning in-state operating entities.Delivers charging-order strength and privacy — not immunity. The operating entity still answers to the state where the property sits.
4 · Domestic asset protection trustsSelf-settled spendthrift trusts in roughly nineteen to twenty-one states, barring creditors after a waiting period of 18 months to 5 years.A non-DAPT-state court applies its own law when the settlor lives there. Toni 1 Trust v. Wacker held Alaska's exclusive-jurisdiction clause cannot stop another state hearing a fraudulent-transfer claim. And §548(e) reaches back ten years regardless of any state waiting period.
5 · Offshore trustsCook Islands and Nevis: non-recognition of US judgments, short limitation periods, a high creditor burden of proof, duress clauses.The contempt record must be disclosed in writing. FTC v. Affordable Media — incarceration affirmed; the assets stayed offshore. In re Lawrence — jailed more than six years; "the impossibility defense is unavailable where the impediment is self-imposed." Retained control and late timing are what put those people in a cell.
6 · Equity strippingRecording a lien in favor of a related entity so property appears encumbered.Courts test economic substance. No loan proceeds advanced, insider beneficiary, lien recorded once litigation was foreseeable, no repayment history — that is a sham, and the deposition question is always "where did the money go?"

Two structures that are routinely oversold

Land trusts provide anonymity of record title and transfer convenience. They provide no liability protection by themselves — the beneficial interest remains an attachable asset. The competent version is a land trust for privacy with an LLC as beneficiary for the shield. Series LLCs save filing costs, but the inter-series shield is untested in non-series states, New York and Pennsylvania do not recognize them, California taxes each series $800 a year, and the IRS has never finalized regulations.

Where the real leverage is

Veil piercing turns on unity of interest: commingling, treating entity assets as personal, failure to maintain records, undercapitalization, shell usage. Entity maintenance is the determinant of whether any of this survives, clients universally fail at it, and it is unambiguously not legal advice. That is why maintenance — not formation — is the product this division should be built around.

What the attorney side costs — so you can price beside it

A single LLC formation runs roughly $500–$2,000 in attorney fees plus state filing. A holding structure with two to four subsidiaries and operating agreements commonly runs $3,500–$10,000. Irrevocable trusts run $3,500–$12,000. A DAPT runs $10,000–$15,000 to set up plus $2,000–$5,000 a year in required in-state trustee fees. An offshore Cook Islands or Nevis structure runs around $21,000–$26,000 all in, with roughly $5,000–$6,000 annual trustee fees and $2,000–$3,000 a year in CPA compliance for Forms 3520, 3520-A and FBAR.

Price the coordination on what you can itemize

Coordinator and consultant fees in this space run from $1,500–$5,000 flat planning fees up to $10,000–$25,000 "asset protection programs" with the legal work subcontracted at a fraction. That spread is precisely what a civil investigative demand asks about. Price on demonstrable non-legal value — the inventory, the exposure map, the insurance review, the packet, the filings, the maintenance — and be able to itemize every hour of it.

Planning estimates at settings you chose — not forecasts. Ongoing maintenance packages in the market run roughly $250–$1,500 a year for review plans and $500–$2,500 for entity compliance, which is where the maintenance slider ranges come from.

09 — Guardrails

The Compliance Spine

This division is regulated by state bars, state attorneys general, insurance departments and securities regulators simultaneously. The AG is the biggest risk, because harm is presumed per transaction.

RuleWhat it means operationallyWhere it lives in the build
Unauthorized practice of lawNo drafting, no structure selection, no explaining legal effect, no applying law to facts, no quasi-legal titles. Michigan's general test: UPL occurs when a person "says or does something on behalf of another involving legal discretion or legal decisions." Selling a blank form is not UPL; advising which form, or how to complete it, is.SENTRY's rule set, with veto power over every client-facing output
Fee-sharing
Model Rule 5.4		A lawyer may not share legal fees with a non-lawyer, and may not let a person who recommends, employs or pays them direct their professional judgment. No percentage of legal fees, no per-referral payments tied to a matter, no marketing agreement whose compensation is a function of clients generated.Compensation logic that structurally cannot reference a legal fee
Referral compensation
Model Rule 7.2(b)		Permitted: non-exclusive, disclosed, uncompensated reciprocal referral arrangements; co-hosted seminars with proportionally split costs; fixed market-rate payment for genuine non-legal services. Prohibited: anything of value for a recommendation.Written referral protocol, no money moving in either direction
"Of counsel" is not a referral labelABA Formal Op. 90-357 requires a close, regular, personal relationship — not a single-case association or a forwarder-receiver arrangement. And an of-counsel lawyer is "associated in" the firm, so conflicts impute in both directions. Multiple firms sharing one of-counsel lawyer become one firm for conflict purposes.Never used as a label for the attorney network
Law-related services
Model Rule 5.7		A firm-affiliated non-legal company must operate in circumstances distinct from legal services, with written notice to the customer that the services are not legal services and that privilege and loyalty protections do not apply. Separate entity, separate branding, separate engagement letters.The engagement letter and the disclosure, delivered before intake
Insurance licensingA producer licence is required in every state of sale. Under the NAIC Producer Licensing Model Act, an unlicensed referral source may be paid only if they do not sell, solicit or negotiate — and in many states only a nominal fixed fee not contingent on a sale. All fifty states now apply the NAIC best-interest standard to annuity recommendations, with care, disclosure, conflict and documentation duties.Licensing register, and best-interest documentation on every annuity file
SecuritiesCompensation for advising on securities can trigger investment adviser registration. Interests in LLCs, funds or structured "programs" can themselves be securities where the investor is passive and relies on a promoter's efforts.Any program-style offering reviewed by securities counsel before it exists
Elder financial protectionTrusted contact collection, temporary holds on suspected exploitation of adults 65+ or impaired adults, mandatory reporting in most states, and elder-abuse statutes that commonly carry enhanced or treble damages plus attorney's fees. This is why AGs pair UPL counts with elder-abuse counts.Intake protocol, escalation path, and training with signed acknowledgement

The nine patterns regulators pattern-match on — treat each as a design prohibition

Free seminars in senior centers, churches or assisted living. A complete financial inventory collected under the banner of planning, followed by a product pitch. Non-lawyer staff with quasi-legal titles giving individualized recommendations. A single captive attorney who never meets clients, paid by the company rather than the client, with fees routed through an intermediary. Templates with no state-law customization. Pressure to sign at the first meeting. Compensation weighted to the product rather than the plan. Marketing built on fear. And claims that a structure makes someone judgment-proof.

What enforcement actually costs

California's Family First matter settled at $7.2 million after the AG sought more than $40 million in civil penalties and $70 million in restitution, with the entities permanently shut down. The Alliance for Mature Americans action ended in a permanent injunction barring the principals from preparing living trusts or advising on estate planning at all. Michigan issued a permanent injunction against a document-prep franchise whose special-needs trust would have disqualified the beneficiary from government benefits. Criminal exposure runs from a misdemeanor in California to a third-degree felony in Florida — but the civil route is the real risk, because unfair-competition statutes count each client contract as a separate violation.

This section summarizes professional-responsibility rules and enforcement history for operational planning. It is not legal advice, and this document does not create an attorney-client relationship. Have a lawyer licensed in your jurisdiction review your architecture, your compensation structure, your engagement letters, and your marketing before you take a client.

12 — Closing

Closing

What this audit is really saying

Most people who try to build this business get one of two things wrong. They cross the legal line because nobody ever drew it, or they stay so far from it that they end up selling nothing but introductions. The version that works draws the line precisely, then builds a real business on the correct side of it.

Insurance first. Timing screened. Exposure mapped as fact. Counsel engaged directly by the client. Filings executed on written instruction. And then maintenance — annual, recurring, unambiguously yours, and the single thing that determines whether any of the structures hold up. That is a defensible division. And with Arizona ABS on the table, it might be a considerably larger one than the wall would ordinarily allow.
