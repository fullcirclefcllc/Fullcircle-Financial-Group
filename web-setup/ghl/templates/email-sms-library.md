# Email & SMS Library — Agency OS

**Voice:** Education-first. No hype. Protection before product.

Replace `[Agency]`, `[Agent]`, `[Calendar]`, `[FirstName]`.

---

## Speed-to-Lead (all product funnels)

**SMS 0 min**  
`[FirstName] — this is [Agent] at [Agency]. Got your request. I’ll text a 15-min link. Reply STOP to opt out.`

**SMS +5 min if no reply**  
`Here’s the calendar: [Calendar]. If now isn’t good, what’s a better window today?`

**Email 0 min — Subject:** Your protection review is booked when you pick a time  

Body: Confirm what they asked for (term / whole / IUL). One paragraph on next step. Calendar button. TCPA footer.

---

## Term — Quote Presented (Day 1 / 3 / 7)

**Day 1 subject:** Your term quote — [Face] for [Premium]/mo  
**Day 3 subject:** One question on the quote  
**Day 7 subject:** Quote window + what happens if we wait  

Day 7 value: income-replacement worksheet or case card (no fabricated client names unless approved).

---

## IUL — After Illustration Presented

**Email:** Walk through guaranteed vs non-guaranteed in three bullets. Ask them to reply with questions before application. Never say “guaranteed growth.”

**SMS:** `Illustration recap is in your inbox. When you’re ready for app paperwork, reply YES and I’ll send the checklist.`

---

## Application Submitted (client)

`Your [Product] application is with [Carrier]. Typical underwriting: [X] days. We’ll update you at exam / APS / decision. Don’t cancel existing coverage until the new policy is in force.`

---

## Policy Placed

Email + SMS: policy number, premium method, annual review date, “do not skip premium” warning for IUL/WL. Tag `stage:placed`. Schedule 11-month review.

---

## No-Show Recovery

**SMS +15 min:** `Missed you — still want the protection review? Here’s a new link: [Calendar].`  
**Email Day 1:** Reschedule + 2-sentence recap of why they booked.  
**Day 3:** Final reschedule; then nurture (not spam).

---

## Agent Onboarding (7 emails / 14 days)

1. Welcome + login + Academy link  
2. Platform Ready (CRM tour)  
3. Compliance cert due  
4. Term Foundations  
5. How we measure you (conversations, speed-to-lead, placement, persistency — not just premium)  
6. First live lead rules  
7. 30-day check-in agenda  

---

## Agency Partner (after agreement)

1. Snapshot imported — admin login  
2. Branding checklist (logo, domain, carriers)  
3. Certify your first three agents  
4. Week 4 production review  
5. Day 85: 90-day success call  

---

## License Expiring

**90 / 60 / 30 days** to agent + compliance role.  
`Your [State] license expires [Date]. Production pause if not renewed. Upload renewal in your agent portal.`

---

## Lapse / At-Risk Save

**SMS:** `Your [Carrier] policy is in grace. Call [Agent] today so the death benefit stays in force: [Phone].`  
Escalate to manager if no contact in 48 hours.

---

## Weekly KPI Scorecard (Monday 7am — Agency Owner)

- Premium MTD vs goal  
- Apps submitted / placed / placement ratio  
- 13-month persistency (book)  
- Conversations last week (team)  
- Licensing expiring 30 days  
- Open NIGO + compliance alerts  
- New agents / agencies this month  

---

## SMS Compliance

- A2P 10DLC registered  
- STOP / HELP language on first SMS  
- Quiet hours 8pm–8am local  
- No SMS to DNC or missing TCPA checkbox
