# SOP — Asset Protection Review

**Pillar:** 2 — Protection  
**Owner:** Breyon Miller  
**Version:** 1.0

---

## Overview
Map all client assets, identify exposure, and implement structural separation to shield personal wealth from business liability.

## Step 1 — Asset Inventory
Complete the financial snapshot (`02_Clients/_templates/financial-snapshot.md`):
- Real estate (primary, investment)
- Vehicles
- Business equity
- Bank / investment accounts
- Life insurance cash value
- Retirement accounts

## Step 2 — Exposure Assessment
Identify what is currently exposed:
- [ ] Home address listed on business filings?
- [ ] Client name on LLC as public record?
- [ ] Personal credit tied to business obligations?
- [ ] Personal assets in client's name with no entity separation?
- [ ] No operating agreement = default state rules = potential exposure

## Step 3 — Recommend Structure
Based on exposure level:

| Exposure Level | Recommendation |
|---|---|
| Low (starting out) | Single-member LLC in home state |
| Medium (some assets, one business) | LLC + Holding LLC (Wyoming) |
| High (real estate, multiple businesses) | Holding LLC + Operating LLC + Real Estate LLC + Trust |

## Step 4 — Implement Entity Separation
See: `LLC-formation-SOP.md` and `holding-company-SOP.md`

## Step 5 — Address Privacy
- Move registered agent to third-party (not home address)
- Consider Wyoming or New Mexico holding company for member privacy
- Remove home address from any public business filings

## Step 6 — Business Insurance Review
- General liability
- E&O (errors & omissions) if applicable to profession
- Umbrella policy
