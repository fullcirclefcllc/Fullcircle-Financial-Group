5 · Guardrails

Keep it clean

Trusts are legal documents. Drafting a trust or giving legal advice about one is the practice of law. Have a licensed estate attorney draft and sign off on every trust. Arizona also certifies Legal Document Preparers; confirm with your attorney what they may prepare before relying on one.
Sell the outcome, not fearExplain what each layer does and doesn't do. Not every client needs a holding company; recommend the smallest structure that fits.
Fund the trustA trust only protects what it owns. Assign the holding company's membership interest to the trust and document it.
Separate everythingEach entity needs its own EIN, bank account, books and signed agreements, or the protection weakens.
Tax and asset-protection claimsHave a CPA or attorney approve any savings or protection language before it goes into ads or the portal.
