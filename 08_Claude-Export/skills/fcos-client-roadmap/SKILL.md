# Full Circle OS: Client Roadmap

Turns one strategy session into a roadmap inside Full Circle OS. Breyon reviews it before the client sees it.

## Input
Session notes, a call transcript, or the Strategy Intake (goal, entity status, 12-month goal, insurance, dependents), plus any credit or funding details shared. If key facts are missing, list them as open questions; never guess them.

## Step 1: Where they stand (one line each, from the facts given)
- **Structure:** entity? holding company? estate documents?
- **Leverage:** personal credit range, business credit started?, funding need and use.
- **Protection:** coverage for income, family, partners.
- **Scale:** systems and marketing in place.

## Step 2: Systems, in order, with the reason
Use the Full Circle OS systems: Launch, Personal Credit, Business Credit, Funding, Protection, Wealth & Legacy, Growth.
Default order (change only for a stated reason):
1. No entity -> Launch first (recommend the Wyoming holding company if privacy matters or they are operating or holding assets).
2. Personal credit blocking goals -> Personal Credit alongside or before Business Credit.
3. Business Credit once the entity, address, phone, email and bank account are consistent.
4. Funding only when the profile is ready.
5. Protection once there is income or a family or partners to protect; licensed agent only.
6. Wealth & Legacy when profitable or holding assets; attorney and CPA engaged directly by the client.
7. Growth when they are ready to run their own client operations.

## Step 3: 30 / 60 / 90 days
Three short lists of concrete steps, each with who does it (client, Full Circle team, or partner professional).

## Step 4: What we'll track for them
2-4 client-level measures (for example: fundability checklist complete, monitoring active, business accounts reporting), each with how it's checked.

## Output
- **Internal version:** everything above, plus open questions, upsell timing and risks.
- **Client-facing version:** plain, encouraging, under one page; no internal notes; prices as [PRICE]. Run it through the `fcfg-compliance-gate` skill and apply its rewrites.

## Rules
- No promised scores, approvals, funding amounts or timelines.
- No legal, tax or investment advice; point to their attorney or CPA.
- Credit repair: no work or billing until the agreement's 3-business-day cancellation period has passed; billing only after work is done.
- Never create, send or schedule anything in GHL; Breyon reviews first.
