# Market Intelligence — Research

## Primary Target Markets

### 1. Real Estate Agents (Primary Niche)
- Income: $100K–$500K gross, heavy 1099
- Pain: Self-employment tax, no business structure, income volatility, no liquidity
- Entry point: "Hidden Cost of Success" presentation
- Common situation: No LLC, home address on business filings, personal credit used for business

### 2. High-Income Professionals ($150K–$300K+)
- W2 or 1099 — doctors, consultants, contractors, coaches, entrepreneurs
- Pain: Maximum tax bracket with no strategy, no asset protection, no legacy plan

### 3. Business Owners (Scaling)
- Revenue $250K–$2M+
- Pain: Personal and business credit mixed, no holding structure, no capital access strategy
