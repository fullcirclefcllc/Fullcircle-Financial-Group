# FCFG business context (from Claude)

What Claude has on file about Full Circle Financial Group, as of Nov 2, 2026. Client files are not included.

## Company
- Full Circle Financial Group (FCFG), Phoenix. Founder and Financial Architect: Breyon Miller.
- Four pillars: Protection (life insurance/IUL), Structure (entities, trusts, estate planning), Leverage (personal and business credit), Scale (systems, marketing, AI automation).
- Full Circle Financial Agency (FCFA) is the insurance distribution arm. Carriers: North American, F&G, National Life Group. Licensed to sell life insurance in Arizona.
- Business model: credit repair as the front door, entity structuring as the high-ticket anchor, insurance as recurring back-end revenue, business credit as the ascension path.
- Origin: founded to teach financial literacy to people with no plan, especially in urban and minority communities, then grew into protection, asset protection and "become your own bank" life insurance — the "full circle."

## Offers and fees
- Funding placement: success fee of 5–10% of the funding amount, capped at $10k. Credit and funding are the marketing hook.
- Credit repair tiers: $300 (only when FCFG is the client's real estate agent), $950, $1,150, $1,500. The $1,150 and $1,500 tiers include aged tradelines and credit monitoring.
- Pay-per-delete option: clients pay only for deleted items.
- Clean Slate Program (standalone, expedited, 45–60 days): $1,850 and $2,200 (Priority).
- Positioned as a document preparation company that files and disputes on the client's behalf using consumer law.
- Clients enroll in monitoring through the SmartCredit affiliate link (smartcredit.com/fccc).
- Client intake: Typeform (hidden fields: package, source, agent).
- LLC offering (Nov 2026): bundle business address, phone, GoDaddy domain and business email in one flow, positioned against Incfile, Swyft and LegalZoom. Wyoming holding company recommended for privacy, via Wyoming Agents.

## Full Circle OS (platform)
- A business operating system ("the QuickBooks of financial services"): entity and holding company setup, business and personal credit, funding, life insurance, with tax and estate planning as add-ons, sold for a platform fee.
- Each service runs as its own system inside the OS; clients buy more systems from inside it. Other entrepreneurs can run their own clients on it.
- To be run from its own LLC. Built first in GoHighLevel to sell now, then a custom Next.js + Supabase app with Claude doing as much of the build as possible before a developer takes over. The code is meant to live in a **private** GitHub repo.

## Goals
- Revenue goal: $50K/month ($1M/year).
- Go-to-market: a one-to-many webinar driven by paid ads; aggressive push on business credit, personal credit and funding (highest revenue and margin).
- Investor pitch: $250K raise, credit as the simple entry point, generational wealth as the big picture.
- AI agent fleet to run parts of the company.
