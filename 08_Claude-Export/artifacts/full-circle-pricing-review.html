Fix before you raise prices

Six things the research turned up

Two are legal requirements, not pricing opinions. Confirm the details with your attorney before you change contracts.

Legal · highest priority

You can't collect credit repair fees up front

The federal Credit Repair Organizations Act says a credit repair company may not charge or receive money for a service "before such service is fully performed." Arizona law (A.R.S. 44-1703) says the same. That applies whatever the fee is called: enrollment, setup, or document prep.

  • Keep your prices, but bill after the work: for example, after each completed dispute round, or monthly in arrears.
  • Pay Per Delete already fits this rule. It can be your default offer.
  • Arizona also requires a surety bond filed with the Corporation Commission. It's $5,000 if you take in under $100,000 a year, and goes up to $25,000 as revenue grows.
  • CROA also requires a written contract, a 3-day cancellation right, and a required disclosure statement before signing.
Cost · high

Tradeline cost is budgeted too low

Complete carries $180 of hard cost and Elite carries $260, and both promise aged tradelines. Retail authorized-user tradelines run about $275 to $1,750 each. A 3-year-old line with a $3,000 limit is about $275. A 10-year-old line with a $45,000 limit is about $1,200.

  • If you buy tradelines retail, every Complete or Elite sale with a good tradeline loses money or barely breaks even.
  • Either price the tradeline in (see recommendations) or list it as a pass-through add-on at your cost plus a markup.
Legal · check

Trusts and wills need an attorney or a certified document preparer

Your $1,500 estate hard cost suggests an attorney already drafts the documents. Keep it that way. In Arizona, non-lawyers who prepare legal documents for others generally need to be certified legal document preparers. Your price sits at the top of the Arizona market ($2,500 to $5,000 for a full plan), which is fine when funding help and follow-through are included.

Legal · check

Get the insurance design fee in writing

Arizona lets a producer charge a fee on top of commission only when the fee and the services it covers are disclosed and agreed to in writing by the client, and the amount is reasonably related to the work (A.R.S. 20-465). Your $2,000 fee is below the average one-time financial plan fee of about $2,900, so the price is fine. Put a one-page fee agreement in front of every design client.

Cost

Entity hard cost may miss Nevada state fees

A Nevada LLC costs $425 in state fees to form ($75 articles, $150 initial list, $200 business license) and $350 a year after that. Wyoming is $100 to form and $60 a year minimum. A Nevada operating company plus a Wyoming holding company is about $525 in state fees plus registered agents, before any labor. Your $450 hard cost doesn't cover that.

  • Raise the hard cost to about $750, or bill state fees at cost as a separate line.
Pricing

Tax strategy and irrevocable trusts are your biggest gaps

Small-business tax planning engagements start around $3,000 to $5,000, and you charge $1,800 with $500 going to the tax partner. Arizona irrevocable trusts run $5,000 to $10,000 or more, and your retail price is $3,800. These two services leave the most money on the table.
