# Case Study — Business Credit Build

**Client type:** Business owner, service industry, 1099  
**Timeline:** 90 days  
**Package:** Business Credit System

---

## The Problem

Client was funding business expenses on personal credit cards — mixing books, killing debt ratios, and leaving $0 in accessible business capital. Every equipment purchase or slow month meant a personal guarantee and personal credit hit.

---

## The System We Built

| Phase | Action | Result |
|-------|--------|--------|
| Days 1–30 | Entity formation + EIN + business bank + Tier 1 vendor accounts | $15K vendor credit |
| Days 31–60 | Tier 2 business credit cards (no PG target) | $35K in cards |
| Days 61–90 | Tier 3 LOC applications + paydex optimization | $70K+ total |
