# Referral Process

**FullCircle Financial Group**

---

## Referral Philosophy
The fastest growth channel for FCFG is a client who got results and tells their network. Every client should leave with a clear understanding of who else we serve.

## When to Ask for a Referral
- At the 90-day milestone call (after first wins are visible)
- At the 12-month review
- Any time a client expresses satisfaction ("this changed everything")

## Referral Script (Conversational)

> "I'm glad this has been valuable for you. A lot of my best clients come from referrals — if you know any other [real estate agents / business owners / high earners] who are paying too much in taxes or don't have a structure behind their income, I'd love to be introduced. You can just send them my way and I'll take care of everything."

## Referral Partner Program

| Tier | Who | Incentive |
|---|---|---|
| Client Referral | Past/current client | $_____ credit or gift |
| Professional Partner | Realtor, mortgage broker, CPA | Revenue share or fee |
| Strategic Alliance | Brokerage, association | Joint presentation / co-marketing |

## Referral Intake

When a referral comes in:
1. Note who referred them in the intake form (`02_Clients/_templates/client-intake-form.md`)
2. Thank the referrer (personal message or gift within 48 hours)
3. Track referral source in client folder
