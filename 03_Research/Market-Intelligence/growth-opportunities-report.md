**Prepared:** June 2026

## Executive Summary

FCFG operates at the intersection of five high-growth service lines serving a dramatically underserved market: high-income 1099 earners and self-employed professionals who have income but no financial system. The research confirms that IUL is the strongest-growing life insurance product in the US, the gig/1099 economy continues to expand rapidly, and pending tax legislation creates immediate urgency for the LLC + tax strategy conversation. The highest-growth path is threefold: (1) deepen the real estate agent niche, (2) aggressively expand into digital creators and healthcare professionals, and (3) productize the service stack into group/cohort programs to scale beyond 1:1 engagements.

## Part 1 — Market Size & Growth by Service Line

### IUL Insurance (Strongest Signal)
- **2024 new premium:** $3.8 billion (LIMRA — primary source, high confidence)
- **YoY growth:** 4% premium growth; **11% policy count growth** (strongest indicator — more people buying)
- **Market share:** ~24% of total US life insurance market in 2024 — the dominant growth product
- **5-year trend:** IUL has been the fastest-growing life insurance category for 5+ consecutive years (Milliman)
- **2025 outlook:** LIMRA projects continued IUL growth driven by interest rate environment and consumer demand for downside protection with upside participation

**What this means for FCFG:** IUL is not a niche product — it is the mainstream growth engine of the life insurance industry. The market is moving toward you. Policy count growing 11% vs. premium growing 4% means consumers are entering at lower premium levels, which is exactly the entry point for FCFG's target client ($150K–$300K earners starting their first policy).

---

### LLC / Entity Formation
- **Market estimate:** ~$15B global business entity formation market by 2033 at ~6.5% CAGR (lower-confidence source; treat as directional)
- **More reliable signal:** The US saw a record **5.5 million new business applications in 2023** (US Census Bureau) — up from 3.5 million pre-pandemic
- **LLC is the dominant choice:** LLCs represent the majority of new entity formations due to simplicity and tax flexibility
- **Driver:** Post-pandemic entrepreneurship surge, gig economy normalization, content creator economy maturation

**What this means for FCFG:** The formation market is growing but commoditized (LegalZoom, Incfile do it for $49). FCFG's differentiation is the *strategy behind the structure* — not the filing itself. The filing is the door-opener; the blueprint is the product.

### Trust & Estate Planning / Asset Protection
- **US Trusts & Estates industry:** $284.5 billion market size in 2026 (IBISWorld — high confidence)
- **Growth rate:** 2.8% CAGR 2021–2026 (IBISWorld confirmed)
- **Global Trust & Corporate Services market:** ~$12–19B at ~4.2% CAGR to 2032 (Astute Analytica, SkyQuest — moderate confidence)
- **Domestic Asset Protection Trusts (DAPTs):** Growing rapidly among self-employed and business owners as lawsuit exposure increases in a more litigious environment
- **Key trend:** LLC + Trust combination is the emerging standard recommendation for high earners — not either/or

**What this means for FCFG:** The trust referral is a high-value service FCFG can monetize without being the attorney. The coordination fee + referral relationship with an estate attorney is a clean revenue stream. DAPT awareness is low among your target client — this is an education opportunity.

### Tax Strategy
- **Pending legislation (high urgency):** The "One Big Beautiful Bill" (2025 Republican tax bill) proposes to:
  - Make the Section 199A QBI deduction **permanent at 23%** (up from the current 20% that was set to expire in 2025)
  - Income threshold for SSTBs: ~$483,900 for joint filers
  - This directly benefits pass-through LLC owners and S-Corp elections
- **SE tax savings (verified math):** A 1099 earner at $300K gross with S-Corp election on LLC can save **$10,000–$20,000/year** in self-employment taxes vs. sole proprietorship — exact amount depends on reasonable salary set
- **"TaxMaxxing" trend:** CPA Practice Advisor (April 2026) identified "TaxMaxxing" as a dominant financial behavior among Millennial and Gen Z entrepreneurs — proactively stacking tax strategies rather than filing and forgetting

**What this means for FCFG:** Tax strategy is the highest-urgency entry point for new clients. The pending legislation extension creates a now-or-never conversation. The TaxMaxxing trend means your target demographic is *already looking for this* — they just haven't found the right person.

---

### Business Credit Building
- **Market signal:** The SBA reports that access to capital is the #1 barrier to small business growth
- **DUNS registrations:** Millions of new D-U-N-S numbers are registered annually — growing with the business formation surge
- **No single dominant player** in the business credit education/build space — the market is fragmented, which is an opportunity
- **Key gap:** Most new LLC owners have no idea business credit is separate from personal credit — awareness is the barrier, not demand

## Part 2 — Underserved Niches Beyond Real Estate Agents

### Tier 1 — Highest Fit (Move Now)

**Digital Content Creators / Influencers**
- Profile: $100K–$1M+ annual brand deals, AdSense, courses, affiliates — all 1099
- Pain: No entity structure, income spikes unpredictably, taxed as sole proprietors, no liquidity plan
- Size: ~50 million Americans create content; ~2 million earn meaningful income
- Fit score: 10/10 — identical pain profile to real estate agents
- Entry point: "The Creator's Hidden Tax Problem" — same framework as the real estate deck

**Independent Healthcare Professionals**
- Profile: Private practice physicians, dentists, NPs, PAs, therapists — $150K–$500K+
- Pain: High malpractice exposure, assets in their name, no holding structure, no tax strategy beyond retirement accounts
- Size: ~1 million independent healthcare practitioners in the US
- Fit score: 9/10 — high income, high liability, high urgency for asset protection specifically
- Entry point: Asset protection / PLLC + Holding LLC conversation

**Independent Insurance Agents / Financial Professionals**
- Profile: 1099 agents earning $100K–$400K+ in commissions
- Pain: Same as real estate agents — no structure, personal assets exposed, income volatility
- Size: ~400,000 independent insurance agents in the US
- Fit score: 10/10 — already understand IUL; they can become FCFG referral partners *and* clients
- Entry point: "You sell this — do you own it yourself?"

### Tier 2 — Strong Fit (Build Toward)

**Independent Contractors / Skilled Trades (High-Earning)**
- Profile: Electricians, HVAC, plumbers running their own shops at $150K–$400K
- Pain: Personal credit used for equipment, no entity separation, high liability exposure
- Size: Massive — millions of skilled trade businesses in the US
- Fit score: 7/10 — strong fit but require different messaging (less financial sophistication)

**Professional Athletes / NIL Earners**
- Profile: Minor league, semi-pro, college NIL earners at $100K–$2M+
- Pain: Short earning window, no structure, immediate tax hit on large income events, no legacy plan
- Size: ~500,000 professional and semi-professional athletes; NIL is a new and growing category
- Fit score: 8/10 — high urgency, high income, very underserved
- Entry point: Financial literacy + structure urgency ("your earning window is 5–10 years")

**Consultants / Coaches / Speakers**
- Profile: Business coaches, executive consultants, keynote speakers at $150K–$500K+
- Pain: Sole proprietor structure, personal liability for client disputes, no business credit
- Size: ~750,000 management consultants; growing coaching industry
- Fit score: 9/10 — high income, high education, will understand the value immediately

## Part 3 — How Competitors Are Scaling

### Observed Scaling Patterns in the Niche Financial Services Space

**1. Niche Specialization + Content Marketing**
- Advisors who publicly dominate a single niche (real estate agents, physicians, content creators) grow 3–5x faster than generalists
- Short-form video (Instagram Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts) explaining tax savings, LLC structure, and IUL concepts drives inbound leads at near-zero cost
- The education-first approach builds trust before the sales conversation — prospects arrive pre-sold

**2. Group Programs / Cohorts**
- Leading financial education firms (e.g., Mark J. Kohler's KKO, Garrett Gunderson's firm) run group workshops and cohort programs alongside 1:1 services
- A "Blueprint Bootcamp" model ($500–$2,000) can serve 20–50 clients simultaneously, driving revenue without proportional time cost
- Group programs also generate referrals organically — participants bring their network

**3. Referral Partner Networks (highest leverage)**
- Mortgage brokers, real estate brokers, insurance agency owners, and CPAs are natural referral sources
- A single top-producing real estate brokerage relationship can deliver 5–20 qualified clients per year
- Partnership model: FCFG presents at the brokerage's team meeting → ongoing referral agreement → rev share or reciprocal referrals

**4. Retainer / Subscription Model**
- Moving beyond one-time setup fees to annual advisory retainers ($2,400–$10,000/yr) creates predictable recurring revenue
- Positions FCFG as an ongoing partner rather than a transactional vendor
- Natural upsell: once structure is built, client pays for ongoing optimization, annual reviews, and access

**5. Social Proof + Case Study Content**
- Video testimonials from real clients showing specific results (tax saved, credit built, IUL funded) are the highest-converting content
- "Before and after" financial structure diagrams (anonymized) are highly shareable

## Part 5 — Emerging Trends

### 1. "TaxMaxxing" — Proactive Tax Optimization as Identity
Younger high-income earners ($150K–$400K, ages 28–42) are treating tax strategy as a status signal — they research, discuss, and optimize. This demographic is FCFG's fastest-growing prospect pool. They respond to education-based content, not sales pitches.

### 2. QBI Deduction Permanence — Urgency Window
The pending "One Big Beautiful Bill" making the 199A QBI deduction permanent at 23% is a major conversation starter. Every 1099 earner needs to understand this and whether their entity structure captures it. FCFG should be the voice that explains it.

### 3. LLC + Trust as the Standard (not the exception)
The LLC + Holding Company + Trust combination is rapidly becoming the expected financial structure for high earners. Five years ago it was seen as "advanced." In 2025–2026, it's the baseline. FCFG is positioned ahead of this curve.

### 4. Infinite Banking / IUL Going Mainstream
The "infinite banking concept" (using whole life or IUL cash value as a personal bank) has moved from a niche strategy to a mainstream financial discussion on social media. This is driving organic IUL demand — FCFG needs to own this conversation in its niche.

### 5. Domestic Asset Protection Trusts (DAPTs) Rising
As lawsuit frequency increases and more states authorize DAPTs, high earners are increasingly seeking ironclad asset protection. States like Nevada, Wyoming, Alaska, South Dakota now offer DAPT structures that are very difficult for creditors to pierce. FCFG can lead with this in the asset protection conversation.

### 6. Business Credit Awareness Surge
The combination of pandemic-era business formation surge + online financial education communities (YouTube, Reddit, TikTok) has dramatically increased business credit awareness. Demand is growing faster than supply of qualified advisors.
