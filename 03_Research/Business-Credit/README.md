# Business Credit — Research

## Credit Building Tier System

### Foundation (Before Applying)
- EIN obtained
- Business address (not home address)
- Business phone number (listed)
- Business bank account open (minimum 30–60 days)
- D-U-N-S number registered (free at dnb.com)
- Business listed in 411 / Dun & Bradstreet

### Tier 1 — Net-30 Vendor Accounts
Vendors that report to D&B, Experian Business, or Equifax Business.
