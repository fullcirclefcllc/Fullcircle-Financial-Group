## Product Mix Strategy (What Leaders Track)

Top agencies do not let agents stay "term only." They coach toward:

```
Term Life        → Entry protection, high volume, low persistency risk if sold correctly
Whole Life       → Permanent protection, cash value, estate planning conversations  
IUL              → Living benefits, tax-free liquidity, retirement supplement
Annuities/FIA    → Retirement income stack (Phase 2 add-on)
```

**Target product mix (mature agency):**
- Term: 30–40% of apps (volume)
- Whole: 20–25% (permanence + CSV)
- IUL: 25–35% (premium + persistency)
- Cross-sell ratio: 40%+ of clients with 2+ product conversations

## Training Model (3 Pillars — Industry Standard)

1. **Scenario-Based Learning** — Anonymized cases, IUL illustration red flags, suitability drills
2. **Compliance First** — NAIC market conduct, state suitability, documentation requirements
3. **Digital Marketing Mastery** — LinkedIn, email drips, local event scripts (templates in GHL)

**Certification gates:**
- Term Certified → can run term pipeline
- Whole Life Certified → can present permanent products
- IUL Mastery → can run IUL illustrations and advanced case design
- Agency Admin → can recruit and manage sub-accounts

## Retention Drivers (Why Agents Stay)

| Factor | Top Agency Practice |
|--------|---------------------|
| Transparent comp | Written vesting, renewal ownership, bonus thresholds |
| Real support | Human case desk, not ticket queue |
| Technology as benefit | Dashboard that saves time, not adds friction |
| Peer network | Community channel, weekly wins call |
| Carrier access | Niche + retirement leaders, maintained appointments |

**Target 2-year agent retention:** 60%+ (industry average ~40%)

## Regulatory Operating Standard

- Monitor NAIC suitability updates — push training within days, not months
- Document every recommendation trail (needs analysis → illustration → application)
- TCPA-compliant outreach (A2P registration in GHL)
- License expiration alerts 90/60/30 days
- E&O and appointment status on every agent record
