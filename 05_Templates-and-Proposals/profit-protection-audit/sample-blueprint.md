## V. Entity Recommendation

### Recommended Structure

```
Thompson Holdings LLC (HoldCo — Wyoming)
    └── Thompson Realty Group LLC (OpCo — Georgia, S-Corp election)
```

### Why This Structure

| Problem | Solution | Outcome |
|---------|----------|---------|
| Personal liability on every transaction | OpCo LLC separates business from personal | Asset shield |
| SE tax on full $385K | S-Corp salary + distributions | $25K+/yr SE tax savings |
| No holding layer for future ventures | HoldCo owns OpCo + future entities | Scalable architecture |
| Commission deposits in personal account | Business bank account under OpCo | Clean books, business credit |
