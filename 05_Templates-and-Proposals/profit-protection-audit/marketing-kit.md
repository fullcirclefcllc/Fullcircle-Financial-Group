**"That's expensive for a diagnostic."**
"I understand. Consider this: if we find $20,000 in tax savings — which happens more often than not — the audit costs you $1,497 and returns $20,000 in the first year alone. That's a 13x return on a one-time investment. If we don't find at least $15K, you don't pay."

**"I need to think about it."**
"I get that. What specifically do you need to think through? Usually it's either timing or whether it's the right fit. I can answer both of those right now."

**"I already have a CPA."**
"Great — I'm not replacing your CPA. What I do is a diagnostic layer that most CPAs don't have time to provide. Your CPA files your taxes; I find what's being left on the table between filings and build you a system to capture it. Most of my clients who have CPAs find $10K–$30K in strategies their CPA never surfaced."

**"I'm not making enough yet."**
"This is actually the perfect time. When you're below $150K, the fixes are simpler and the habits you build now prevent the bleeding that kills most 1099 earners when they scale. I can also offer a scaled version at $997 for clients under $100K gross."

**"Can you just tell me what to do without the audit?"**
"I can give you general guidance — but I can't give you specific numbers without knowing your numbers. The audit is what lets me say 'you specifically need X' instead of 'most people need X.' The specificity is what makes it worth $1,497."
