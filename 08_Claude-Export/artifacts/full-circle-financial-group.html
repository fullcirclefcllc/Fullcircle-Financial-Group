The client path

How a plan comes together

Every client starts at a different point. This is the order we build in.

I

Strategy call

We review your credit, your business and your goals, then map which pillars come first.

II

Clean up personal credit

Dispute inaccurate items, set utilization targets and build the habits that keep scores healthy.

III

Structure the business

Form the right entity, get the EIN, banking and business listings in place so the company stands on its own.

IV

Build business credit and fund

Layer tradelines, then match you with lenders when your profile is ready.

V

Protect and pass it on

Put coverage, trusts and estate documents in place so what you built is protected.
