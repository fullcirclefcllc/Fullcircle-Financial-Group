Compliance and risk

Built to pass a regulator's read.

Credit services are heavily enforced. The model is designed around the rules, not around them.

No advance fees for credit work

The federal Credit Repair Organizations Act bars charging before services are fully performed (15 U.S.C. §1679b(b)). The projections collect credit fees after each round of work, not at signup.

Arizona credit services rules

A.R.S. §44-1701 et seq. covers credit services organizations, including a surety bond of 5% of prior-year fees ($5,000–$25,000) where required. Budgeted.

No guarantees, ever

All marketing passes the FCFG compliance gate: no promised score increases, deletions or approvals, and required disclosures on every page.

Funding disclosures

Success fees are disclosed in writing. States such as California and New York have commercial financing disclosure laws; the platform will meet them before selling there.

Concentration risk

Credit is the entry point. Structure, insurance and software revenue reduce dependence on any one service as the client base matures.

Acquisition cost risk

If paid conversion runs below plan, ad spend is cut back to the level the cash covers. The conservative scenario shows why that gate matters.
