Why you need it

Why build credit in the company's name

A separate profile

Dun & Bradstreet, Experian Business and Equifax Business keep files on companies. A strong business file lets the company be judged on its own record.

Vendor terms

Net-30 terms mean you get the supplies now and pay within 30 days. Paying those accounts on time, or early, is how a payment history forms.

Higher limits over time

As accounts report and age, creditors may offer larger lines. Limits are set by each creditor and are never guaranteed.

Less personal exposure

Some business options may not require a personal guarantee, where lenders allow. Many still do, and we tell you which is which before you apply.

When you need it

When to start building

  • Trigger 1After the foundation is set

    Structure comes first. An LLC in good standing, an EIN, a business bank account and a consistent name, address and phone. Applications on a shaky foundation tend to stall.

  • Trigger 2Before you need the money

    Build while there's no pressure. A profile takes time to form. Starting before a big purchase or a slow season means the file is ready when you are.

  • Trigger 3When you want to protect personal utilization

    Keep business spending off your personal reports. Accounts that report only to the business bureaus don't add to the utilization on your personal reports. Some business cards do report personally, and we check before you apply.

FAQ

Questions clients ask

What is PAYDEX?

PAYDEX is Dun & Bradstreet's payment score for a business, from 1 to 100, based on how promptly the business pays the vendors and creditors that report to it. It's weighted by dollar amount. A score around 80 generally means paid on time; paying early can score higher.

Will building business credit affect my personal credit?

Accounts that report only to the business bureaus don't appear on your personal reports. Some applications include a personal credit check or a personal guarantee, and some business cards report to personal bureaus. We tell you before you apply.

Do I have to sign a personal guarantee?

Often, early on. Some options may not require a personal guarantee, where lenders allow. We flag which accounts require one before you apply.

What is a net-30 account?

A vendor account where you buy now and pay the invoice within 30 days. When the vendor reports to business bureaus, each on-time or early payment becomes part of the company's history.

Do I need revenue to build business credit?

Starter vendor accounts are often available to newer businesses. Store cards, fleet cards and funding usually look more closely at revenue and time in business. Every creditor sets its own criteria.

How long does it take?

It depends on how quickly accounts report and on each creditor's criteria. We don't promise timelines. You get status updates on a regular schedule, including while we wait on a report.
