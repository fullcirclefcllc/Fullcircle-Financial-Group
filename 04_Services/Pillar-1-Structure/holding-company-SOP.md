## Overview
A holding company owns the assets; the operating LLC runs the business. A lawsuit against the operating LLC cannot reach assets held in the holding company.

## When to Recommend a Holding Company

- Client has or plans multiple LLCs
- Client owns real estate
- Client has significant assets to protect
- Client wants maximum anonymity
- Client is scaling and needs a parent entity

## Recommended Structure

```
[Client] Holding LLC (Wyoming)
    ├── [Client] Operating LLC (home state)
    ├── [Client] Real Estate LLC (optional)
    └── IUL Policy / Investments (owned by holding co. or trust)
```

## Step 1 — Form Holding LLC in Wyoming (or Nevada)
- Wyoming preferred: no public member disclosure, strong charging order protection, $60/yr
- File Articles of Organization with Wyoming SOS
- Registered agent in Wyoming required (~$50–$150/yr)

## Step 2 — Form Operating LLC in Home State
- This is where active business is conducted
- Register as foreign LLC in home state if needed (~$100–$300)
- Operating LLC is *owned by* the Holding LLC (not the client personally)

## Step 3 — Update Operating Agreement
- Holding LLC is listed as the sole member of Operating LLC
- Client owns Holding LLC as member

## Step 4 — Transfer Existing Assets (if applicable)
- Consult with attorney before transferring real estate (deed transfer may trigger tax)
- Transfer business assets to operating LLC, real estate to real estate LLC

## Step 5 — Update Insurance
- Ensure business insurance names the correct LLC as insured
- Update any contracts or agreements to reflect new entity names
