## Suitability Gate

- Liquidity needs inside surrender schedule → do not force annuity  
- Existing qualified money: rollover / transfer paperwork complete  
- Client age and state senior rules documented  
- Cross-sell from IUL/Whole only when income-floor is the goal — not to “replace” cash-value life

- Annuity mix % (Phase 2)  
- Average premium  
- Free-look rescission rate (target &lt; 3%)  
- Cross-sell ratio (life + annuity on same household)
