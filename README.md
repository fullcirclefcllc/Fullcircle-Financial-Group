## The 3-Pillar Blueprint

**Pillar 1 — Structure:** LLC formation, Holding Company, entity separation  
**Pillar 2 — Protection:** Asset shielding, trusts, privacy/anonymity  
**Pillar 3 — Capital:** IUL policy, business credit build

---

## Adding a New Client

1. Copy `02_Clients/_templates/` into `02_Clients/[LastName-FirstName]/`
2. Complete `intake.md` during first call
3. Complete `financial-snapshot.md` on onboarding call
4. Start `progress-tracker.md` and track all milestones
5. Store signed agreement and client documents in `documents/`
