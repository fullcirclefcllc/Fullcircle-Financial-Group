# Case Study — Tax Structure Fix

**Client type:** Atlanta realtor, 1099, $385K/year  
**Timeline:** 60 days  
**Package:** Blueprint

---

## The Problem

Marcus was earning $385K in commissions as a sole proprietor. He felt successful — but every slow quarter meant dipping into personal savings. He paid an estimated $152K in total tax with no offset strategy. His home, investments, and commission account sat exposed in his personal name.

> "I was making good money. I just wasn't keeping it."

---

## The System We Built

| Pillar | Action |
|--------|--------|
| **Structure** | HoldCo (WY) + OpCo LLC (GA) with S-Corp election |
| **Protection** | $1.5M IUL + $2M umbrella policy |
| **Leverage** | $750/mo IUL funding + 90-day business credit build |
| **Scale** | Trust referral + quarterly CPA coordination |

---

## The Outcome

| Metric | Before | After (Year 1) |
|--------|--------|----------------|
| Estimated annual tax | $152K | $106K |
| Annual tax savings | — | **$46K** |
| Business credit | $0 | $75K |
| Slow-quarter liquidity | Personal savings | IUL policy loan path |
| Asset exposure | Full personal | Entity-separated |
