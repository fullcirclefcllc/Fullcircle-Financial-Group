Which company first

Start with FCFG and FCFA as one funnel. Park everything else.

With $8,000, the question is which business gets you the most cash back per dollar and per hour. Spread across seven ventures, $8K buys a little of everything and moves none of them. Put into one funnel, it can pay for itself inside 60 days.

Fastest cash (this month)

FCFA insurance, from warm relationships

No ad spend needed. The Barrett group benefits rollout, the Wise family policy waiting on signature, and a "financial review" offered to every current and past credit client. A single IUL typically pays $2,500–$6,500 in first-year commission, more than two or three credit repair files.

Fastest growth (next 12–24 months)

FCFG credit repair as the front door

It is the cheapest client to acquire, the easiest yes, and the only offer with enough demand to buy with ads. Each credit client is a future insurance, structure, and business credit client. Growth comes from that engine. Profit comes from what you sell to those clients next.

The "multi-million" reality check

$50K a month is a $600K-a-year business. That's a real milestone, but it isn't multi-million yet.

Small service businesses usually sell for about 2–3× the owner's yearly profit. At $50K/mo with ~40% margins, that's roughly $0.5–0.7M in value. To be worth $2M+, you need about $170K+/mo in revenue or a large book of recurring insurance renewals. Agency books carry recurring value; credit repair files don't.

This is why FCFA's agent team and overrides are the long-term path to a multi-million valuation. Credit repair is how you get there, not the business you sell.
