## The Core Message

**You've spent 10, 20, 30 years building it. Without an estate plan, one bad day — a stroke, an accident, a sudden illness — means courts decide who gets it, the government takes a cut, and the process is public. Everything you built, exposed and delayed. An estate plan is the final layer of your financial architecture. It makes sure what you built survives you — on your terms.**

---

## Hook Library

**Hook 1 — The Probate Truth**
> "If you die without a trust, your estate goes to probate. That means a court — not you — decides how your assets are distributed. It's public. It's slow (12–24 months). It's expensive (3–8% of estate value in fees). And your family waits. A revocable living trust sidesteps all of this. Everything transfers privately, immediately, exactly as you intended. After 30 years in this industry, I've seen what happens to families without one. It is avoidable."

**Hook 2 — The Beneficiary Gap**
> "Quick question: do you know who the beneficiary is on your life insurance policy? Your 401K? Your IRA? Most people named someone 10 years ago and never updated it. I've seen estates go to ex-spouses, deceased relatives, and the wrong children because nobody audited the beneficiary designations. This is a 30-minute fix that protects your entire financial legacy."

**Hook 3 — The Business Owner Version**
> "Your business is your biggest asset. When you die — or if you become incapacitated — who runs it? Who owns it? Who decides whether to sell it? Without a proper succession plan in your operating agreement and a trust that holds the business equity, the answer is 'whoever the courts decide.' We build the succession structure into your blueprint from the beginning."

**Hook 4 — The Generational Wealth Angle**
> "First generation builds it. Second generation loses it. Third generation has never seen it. That is the American wealth cycle for most families. A trust — structured correctly — can protect what you build across multiple generations. Your children receive it protected from creditors, divorce settlements, and poor decisions. This is what generational wealth actually looks like."

## Estate Planning Checklist (Client-Facing)

Share as a lead magnet, carousel, or email attachment:

**Your Estate Planning Audit — 10 Questions**
1. Do you have a will? Is it current (within 5 years)?
2. Do you have a revocable living trust?
3. Are your major assets titled in the trust?
4. Have you reviewed your beneficiary designations in the last 2 years?
5. Do you have a durable power of attorney for finances?
6. Do you have a healthcare directive / living will?
7. If you have children under 18, is a guardian named?
8. Does your business have a succession clause in the operating agreement?
9. Does your trust align with your IUL/life insurance beneficiary structure?
10. Does your spouse know where everything is and how to access it?

**Score: 0–3 Yes = Urgent | 4–6 Yes = Needs work | 7–10 Yes = Well-positioned**
